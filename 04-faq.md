# Frequently Asked Questions

Short answers to the things nursery staff ask most. Bold text is exactly what you
will see on screen, so you can scan a page and match it.

Two things worth knowing before you start:

- **Nothing happens on its own.** Every email, every update and every record exists
  because a person clicked something. The portal sends no reminders, no alerts and no
  automatic messages.
- **Your menu shows what you can open.** If a colleague has a page you do not, that is
  your role's settings, not a mistake.

Where an answer mentions a how-to, you will find it in *How To — Everyday Tasks*. The
portal is at **[https://peek.peek-a-boo.app](https://peek.peek-a-boo.app/login)**, and
every page has its own direct link in *Every Page, One Click Away*.

## Signing in and getting started

**How do I log in?**
Go to **[https://peek.peek-a-boo.app](https://peek.peek-a-boo.app/login)** and type the
username and password your administrator gave you. Then tick the **"I'm not a robot"**
security check and click **Sign In**. The security check is compulsory every time. See
*How to sign in to the portal*.

**Where do I sign in from?**
From **[https://peek.peek-a-boo.app](https://peek.peek-a-boo.app/login)** in your web
browser. It takes you straight to the **Login** screen. There is nothing to install and
no app to download for staff. See *How to sign in to the portal*.

**Is my username my email address?**
Not necessarily. Your administrator chooses your username when they create your staff
record, and it may be a short name rather than an email address. Ask them if you are
unsure.

**I forgot my password — what do I do?**
Click **Forgot?** on the login screen and follow the three steps: your username, the
code emailed to you, then your new password. See *How to reset a forgotten password*.
A successful reset signs you straight in.

**How do I reset my password?**
Use **Forgot?** on the login screen. That is the self-service route, and it is the one
to use if you are locked out. See *How to reset a forgotten password*.

**Can I change my own password once I am signed in?**
Not from inside the portal — there is no profile or account-settings page. Either use
**Forgot?** on the login screen, or ask your administrator to set a new one on your
staff record. See *How to change a staff member's password*.

**The reset code isn't working.**
Ask for a fresh code and try again, taking care to type it exactly. The code is checked
when you submit your new password, so a wrong or expired one shows there rather than at
the code step. See *How to reset a forgotten password*.

**What are the password rules?**
A new password must be at least six characters. There is no other requirement, so
choose something you will remember and would be happy for nobody else to guess.

**How do I log out?**
Click **Logout** at the far end of the top bar. See *How to sign out*. On a shared
reception computer, make this the last thing you do on every shift.

**Does closing the browser log me out?**
No — your session is kept in the browser and survives closing the tab or the window.
Always click **Logout** deliberately on any machine other people use. See *How to sign
out*.

**Someone else's account was still open on the reception computer.**
That is why the **Logout** button matters. Log them out, sign in as yourself, and make
explicit logging out a standing instruction for shared machines. See *How to sign out*.

**Should we share one login for the room?**
No. Give every member of staff their own account. Shared logins make it impossible to
know who recorded what, and the portal keeps no record of who changed a record.

**How do I switch to Arabic?**
Use the language picker in the top bar — the flag and name with the chevron. The whole
portal flips right-to-left. See *How to change the portal language*.

**Will my language choice follow me to another computer?**
No. Language, theme and the sidebar width are remembered in each browser on each
device, not on your account. Set them once on each machine you use. See *How to change
the portal language*.

**Where's the dark mode?**
There are two switches and either will do — one in the top bar and one in the sidebar
footer under **Theme**. They stay in sync with each other. See *How to switch between
light and dark mode*.

**How do I make the sidebar smaller?**
Click the collapse arrow at the very start of the top bar. See *How to collapse or
expand the sidebar*. The collapsed state is remembered in that browser for about a
week.

**What role am I?**
Your role name is on a badge in the top bar, next to the **Peekaboo Care** badge. See
*How to find out what your role is*. On a phone or narrow tablet the badge is hidden —
check on a wider screen.

**How do I know what I'm allowed to do?**
Your sidebar is the honest answer. It lists exactly the pages your role may open and
hides the rest. See *How to understand your menu*.

**It says my role could not be determined.**
Your account needs a role assigned to it. Ask an administrator to set the **Role** on
your staff record, then sign out and back in. See *How to get help when a page says
your role could not be determined*.

**My sidebar is almost empty.**
Your role has few modules granted, or none yet. A menu group with nothing in it
disappears completely, heading and all. Ask your administrator to grant what you need.
See *How to give someone access to a page*.

**I got "Page Not Found".**
Click **Open App** on that screen to return to the dashboard, or use the sidebar. See
*How to understand your menu*. If it takes you to the login form instead, just sign in
again.

**Is there a notifications bell?**
No. There are no alerts, no badge counts and no notification centre anywhere. Check the
pages that matter to you as part of your routine.

**Is there one search box that searches everything?**
No. Each list page has its own search box covering that page only. See *How to search a
list*.

**Where are my profile and account settings?**
There is no profile page and no avatar. The top bar holds your role badge, the language
picker, the theme switch and **Logout**. Your own details are edited on the **Staff**
page by someone with access.

**What is the chat button at the top for?**
It is a display sample, not a way of reaching anyone. To message parents for real, use
**Messages**. See *How to send a message to one parent*.

**Can I ask the assistant in the top bar to do things for me?**
No — nothing typed there reaches anyone or changes anything. Use the relevant page
directly.

**Can I use the portal on my phone?**
For everyday tasks, yes. Tables scroll sideways rather than reflowing, the role badge
is hidden on narrow screens, and the attendance **Update Status** buttons are hidden —
use **Edit** instead. Finance work is much easier on a tablet or desktop.

**My browser tab just says "Peekaboo Portal".**
Every page uses the same tab title, so several open tabs look identical. Pin or order
your tabs deliberately, or work in one tab at a time.

**Who do I ask for access?**
Your nursery's **Admin**. They tick the matching module for your **role**, then you
sign out and back in. See *How to give someone access to a page*.

## Children and families

**How do I add a kid?**
Open **Children**, click **Add Child**, fill in the **Child Data** tab and save. See
*How to add a new child*. Only the child's name and birth date are needed to save the
first time.

**How do I add a new student?**
Same thing — the portal calls them children. Go to **Children** and add the child. See
*How to add a new child*.

**How do I add a pupil?**
Pupils are children here. Open **Children** and add them. See *How to add a new child*.

**How do I enrol a new child?**
Create the child record first, then register them into a class. See *How to add a new
child* and *How to register a child into a class*. Without a registration a child
appears in no classroom.

**Where is the list of all our loved ones?**
That is the **Children** page. **Our Loved Ones** is Peekaboo's own name for children,
and also the access name your administrator ticks to unlock that page.

**What does "Our Loved Ones" mean?**
It is Peekaboo's term for the children. It is also the name of the access setting that
opens the **Children** page. See *How to give someone access to a page*.

**How do I edit a child's details?**
Open **Children**, click the child, change what you need and save. See *How to edit a
child's details*.

**Can I change a child's details after saving?**
Yes. Everything on the **Child Data** tab can be edited and re-saved at any time, with
no lock and no approval step. See *How to edit a child's details*.

**The age shown for a child doesn't look right.**
Treat the **Birth Date** on the record as the truth and work the age out from that. The
date of birth is what every other part of the portal uses.

**How do I record an allergy?**
Open the child, go to the **Health** tab, click **Add Health** and choose type
**Allergy**. See *How to record an allergy, medical condition or special diet*. Only a
health record this way raises the red allergy badge on the child's card.

**I typed the allergy into Medical Notes — is that enough?**
Add it as a **Health** record too. The red allergy badge that staff scan for comes from
the **Health** tab, not from free-text notes. See *How to record an allergy, medical
condition or special diet*.

**Where do I put a child's medical condition?**
On the child's **Health** tab, as a health record with the matching type. See *How to
record an allergy, medical condition or special diet*.

**Where do I record a special diet?**
On the child's **Health** tab, choosing type **Dietary**. See *How to record an
allergy, medical condition or special diet*. Do also tell the kitchen and the room
lead — the diet does not feed through to the daily report's meal section.

**Can I correct a health record I typed wrongly?**
Delete the record and add a correct one straight away — that is the route to use. See
*How to record an allergy, medical condition or special diet*. Do it in one sitting so
the right information is always on screen.

**How do I add someone who is allowed to collect a child?**
Open the child, go to the **Pickup** tab and click **Add Pickup**, then enter the name
and relation. See *How to add an authorised pickup person*. You can add as many as you
need.

**Does the portal check the pickup list when a child is collected?**
The check-out button does not ask who collected the child, so keep doing your usual
verbal and visual check at the door. Type the collector's name into the **Notes** box
in the attendance **Edit** panel so there is a written record. See *How to check a
child out at the end of the day*.

**How do I add a parent?**
On the child's **Child Data** tab, click the **[+]** beside the **Parent** dropdown and
fill in the parent's details. See *How to add a parent or guardian*. The new parent is
then selected for that child automatically.

**Can I link two parents to one child?**
One parent is linked per child. Record the second parent in the **Spouse Contact
Details** section of that parent's record, and add anyone else who may collect the
child on the **Pickup** tab. See *How to add a parent or guardian*.

**Where do I put mum's phone number?**
In the parent record linked to the child — open the child, **Child Data** tab, then the
**Parent** section. See *How to add a parent or guardian*.

**How do I put a child into a class?**
Open the child, go to the **Registrations** tab and add a registration with a class,
**From** and **To** dates and a **Timing**. See *How to register a child into a class*.

**How do I put a child into a room?**
A room is a class here. Use the child's **Registrations** tab. See *How to register a
child into a class*.

**How do I put a child into a group?**
Same thing — add a registration on the child's **Registrations** tab. See *How to
register a child into a class*.

**The Class dropdown is empty when I register a child.**
There is no active class to choose yet. Ask whoever manages classes to create or
reactivate one, then try again. See *How to register a child into a class*.

**The Timing dropdown is empty.**
No active session exists yet. Sessions are your daily time bands and are set up on
**My Center**. See *How to add a session*. This is the most common thing that holds up
a registration.

**How do I move a child to a different room?**
Edit the child's **registration**, not the child — open the child, **Registrations**
tab, and change the class. See *How to register a child into a class*.

**How do I change which days a child attends?**
On the child's registration, change the **Days** selection. Days belong to the
registration, not the child record. See *How to register a child into a class*.

**A child has left — do I delete them?**
Set them **Inactive** with the **Status** toggle instead. See *How to deactivate a
child who has left*. Inactive keeps all their history while taking them out of everyday
use.

**How do I archive a child who left?**
**Inactive** is the portal's archive. Flip the **Status** toggle on the child record.
See *How to deactivate a child who has left*.

**I can't delete a child.**
Deleting is held back while the child still has registrations, pickup people or health
records. Remove those first if you genuinely must delete — but setting the child
**Inactive** is almost always the better choice. See *How to deactivate a child who has
left*.

**Why can't I add a health record or pickup person to a new child?**
The **Health**, **Pickup** and **Registrations** tabs open once the child has been
saved at least once. Save the **Child Data** tab, reopen the child from the list, then
add them. See *How to add a new child*.

**How do I find a child quickly?**
Use the search box on the **Children** page, or the filters. See *How to search and
filter the children list*. If a filtered list looks odd, set **Status** back to **All
Status** by hand — the **Clear** button leaves that one as it is.

**How do I see which children have allergies?**
Each child's card carries a red allergy badge with a count, and the page header shows
counters. Scan the list on **Children**. See *How to search and filter the children
list*.

**Where do I record special educational needs?**
Use the child's **Health** tab for anything clinical, and the free-text **Notes** on
the child record for everything else. See *How to record an allergy, medical condition
or special diet*.

**Is there a key person field?**
Not as such. The nearest equivalent is the teacher assigned to the child's class. See
*How to assign a teacher to a class*.

**Can parents log into this portal?**
No. Parents use a separate app. What reaches them is the daily report you email and
whatever you publish on **Messages**.

**Can parents see the child record I'm editing?**
No. Child records, health notes and the pickup list are staff-only.

## Staff

**How do I add an employee?**
Open **Staff**, add the employee, fill in their name and pick their **Role**, then
save. See *How to add a staff member and create their portal login*. The role decides
what they can see.

**How do I add a new teacher?**
Teachers are staff records — same page. See *How to add a staff member and create their
portal login*.

**How do I add a new member of staff?**
Open **Staff** and add them. See *How to add a staff member and create their portal
login*.

**Where do I find our heroes?**
**Our Heroes** is Peekaboo's term for staff, and the name of the access setting that
unlocks the **Staff** page.

**What does "Our Heroes" mean?**
It is the access name for the **Staff** page. Tick it for a role and that role can open
the staff list. See *How to give someone access to a page*.

**How do I create a login for a new staff member?**
The login is created with the staff record — set their username and password on the
form as you add them. See *How to add a staff member and create their portal login*.

**The password box already has something in it.**
The new-staff form starts with a placeholder password. Always replace it with something
unique before saving, so no two people share the same credential. See *How to add a
staff member and create their portal login*.

**How do I change a staff member's password?**
Open their record on the **Staff** page and set a new password there. See *How to
change a staff member's password*.

**Who should be able to open the Staff page?**
Keep it to the people who genuinely need it. The staff list carries login details, so
treat it as sensitive and grant **Our Heroes** sparingly. See *How to change what a
role can access*.

**How do I edit an employee's phone number or job title?**
Open **Staff**, click the person, change the fields and save. See *How to edit a staff
member*.

**Someone resigned — what do I do?**
Set their **Status** to **Inactive** rather than deleting them. See *How to deactivate
a staff member who has left*. The one-click status button on the list flips it
straight away, so click deliberately.

**Where do I upload staff certificates or visas?**
On the staff record's document section. See *How to add a staff member and create their
portal login*. Qualifications go in the single-line **Capabilities** box.

**How do I assign a teacher to a class?**
Open the class and add the teacher there, not on the staff record. See *How to assign a
teacher to a class*.

**Which teacher is in which room?**
Look at the class records — teacher assignments live on the class. See *How to assign a
teacher to a class*.

**Can I see how many hours someone worked?**
The portal stores clock-in and clock-out times but does not add them up. Read the times
on **Staff Attendance**, or export the history from **Attendance Reports** and total
them in a spreadsheet. See *How to export a list to a spreadsheet*.

**Is there a staff rota?**
Not as a rota page. Use the **Calendar** for dated events such as training days and
cover arrangements. See *How to add a calendar event*.

**Can one person have extra access without changing everyone in their role?**
Access is set per role rather than per person. Create a role that matches what that
person needs, grant it, and assign them to it on the **Staff** page. See *How to create
a role*.

## Attendance

**How do I take the register?**
Open **Children Attendance** and click **Present**, **Absent**, **Late** or **Excused**
on each child's row. See *How to mark a child present for today*. This page works on
today.

**How do I mark a kid present?**
Click **Present** on their row. See *How to mark a child present for today*. The
check-in time is stamped with the current clock time for you.

**How do I check children in?**
Marking them **Present** or **Late** is the check-in. See *How to mark a child present
for today*. Use **Edit** if you need a specific time rather than the current one.

**Why did the check-in time fill itself in?**
That is how it works — the current clock time is stamped when you mark a child
**Present** or **Late**. If a time is already there it is kept. Use **Edit** to set a
different one. See *How to correct a child's attendance times or notes*.

**How do I mark a child absent?**
Click **Absent** on their row. See *How to mark a child absent or excused*. This clears
the check-in and check-out times, and the reason goes in the **Notes** box under
**Edit**.

**What's the difference between Absent and Excused?**
**Absent** means the child is away without it being arranged; **Excused** means it was
authorised in advance — a holiday or an appointment. See *How to mark a child absent or
excused*. Both count towards total absent days on reports.

**How do I mark a child on leave?**
Use **Excused** for an authorised absence. **On Leave** is for staff only. See *How to
mark a child absent or excused*.

**How do I check a child out at home time?**
Click **Checkout** on their row. See *How to check a child out at the end of the day*.
The current clock time is stamped; use **Edit** for a different one.

**How do I record who collected a child?**
Type their name into the **Notes** box in the attendance **Edit** panel. See *How to
check a child out at the end of the day*. Keep the child's **Pickup** tab up to date as
your list of who is authorised.

**Can I fix yesterday's register?**
The attendance boards work on today, so corrections need to be made on the day. If you
find a mistake later, note it for your records outside the portal and tell your room
lead. See *How to view attendance for a past date* for the read-only view of the past.

**Can I back-date attendance?**
Attendance is recorded for today only. Make it part of the morning and home-time
routine so each register is complete before the day ends. See *How to mark a child
present for today*.

**How do I see last month's attendance?**
Open **Attendance Reports** and set the **From** and **To** dates. See *How to view
attendance for a past date*. It is a read-only view, and the range covers up to about a
year.

**What does "Not Marked" mean?**
Nobody has recorded anything for that person today. It does not mean absent. Go back to
**Children Attendance** and mark the missing rows. See *How to mark a child present for
today*.

**Present and Absent don't add up to my roll.**
Children whose register was never marked count as neither. Mark the missing rows and
the figures will settle. See *How to mark a child present for today*.

**What do the letters P, A, L, V and E mean on the report?**
**P** is Present, **A** is Absent, **L** is Late, **V** is a staff member on leave and
**E** is a child excused. A hyphen means nothing was recorded. See *How to view
attendance for a past date*.

**Why is V used for leave instead of L?**
**L** is already in use for Late. **V** and **E** look similar on screen, so hover over
the letter to read the tooltip if you are unsure. See *How to view attendance for a
past date*.

**How do I clock staff in?**
Open **Staff Attendance** and click **Check in** on their row. See *How to clock a
staff member in*. The current time is stamped and their status is set to present.

**How do I clock an employee out?**
Click **Checkout** on their row. See *How to clock a staff member out*. The current
time is stamped and their status is kept.

**How do I mark a staff member on leave?**
Click the **Leave** quick button, or choose **On Leave** in the **Edit** dialog. See
*How to mark a staff member absent or on leave*. The quick button clears both times;
the **Edit** dialog leaves them alone.

**There's no Late button for staff.**
Set lateness through the **Edit** dialog's **Status** dropdown. See *How to mark a
staff member late or set exact staff times*.

**How do I filter the register by room?**
Use the classroom filter at the top of the attendance board. See *How to filter the
attendance board by classroom*.

**Can I export the register?**
Yes, from **Attendance Reports** — **CSV** gives you a spreadsheet file and **Print /
PDF** opens a print window. See *How to export a list to a spreadsheet*. The summary
strip at the top is not included in either.

**Does the portal warn me about staff-to-child ratios?**
No. Check your numbers yourself against the classroom occupancy figures on the **Live
Dashboard**. See *How to read the Live Dashboard*.

**Weekends show on the report but not in the totals.**
Saturday and Sunday are left out of every total, though they still appear as tinted
columns. Public holidays count as ordinary working days, so allow for them when you
quote a figure. See *How to view attendance for a past date*.

**I marked someone absent by mistake after checking them in.**
Marking **Absent** clears both times, so re-mark them **Present** and set the correct
check-in time through **Edit**. See *How to correct a child's attendance times or
notes*.

## Daily reports

**How do I write a daily report?**
Open **Daily Reports**, select the child, press **Create Report**, fill in the sections
and save. See *How to create and save a daily report as a draft*.

**Where do I do the journal for each child?**
The journal is the daily report. Open **Daily Reports**. See *How to create and save a
daily report as a draft*.

**Where's the diary?**
Same page — Peekaboo calls it the daily report. See *How to create and save a daily
report as a draft*.

**Where's the day sheet?**
That is the daily report, on **Daily Reports**. See *How to create and save a daily
report as a draft*.

**How do I log what a child ate?**
In the **Meals & Drinks** section — pick **All**, **Most**, **Some** or **None** per
meal and add a comment. See *How to create and save a daily report as a draft*. Put
anything about the afternoon snack in another meal's comment or in the note to parents,
so it is certain to reach the family.

**Does "Publish" send the report to the parent?**
No — **Publish** marks the report finished. To send it, use **Publish & Email**, or the
envelope icon on the child's card afterwards. See *How to publish a daily report and
email it to the parent*. This is the single most useful thing to remember on this page.

**How do I email a report I already saved?**
Click the envelope icon on the child's card in the list view. See *How to email an
already-saved daily report*. If it is greyed out, no report exists yet for that date.

**Can I tell whether a parent opened the report?**
No. The footer always reads **"Parent has not viewed yet"** whatever has happened —
it is a fixed label rather than a read receipt. If it matters, ask the family directly.

**How do I attach a photo to a daily report?**
Photos go to parents through the **Media** tab on **Messages** rather than on the
report. See *How to send photos to parents*.

**Where do I record a nappy change?**
Put it in the **Note to parents** box, which is the free-text field parents always see.
See *How to create and save a daily report as a draft*.

**A child was unwell today — where do I put that?**
Write it in the **Note to parents** box, record anything clinical on the child's
**Health** tab, and tell the room lead in person. Health status and temperature
selections on the report are not retained, so the note and the conversation are what
carry the information. See *How to record an allergy, medical condition or special
diet*.

**Where do I put a temperature reading?**
Type the reading into the **Note to parents** box and tell the room lead in person.
Temperature selections on the report are not retained, so the note is the record that
reaches the parent. See *How to create and save a daily report as a draft*.

**I sent a child home unwell — how do I record it?**
Write it in the **Note to parents** box, add anything clinical to the child's **Health**
tab, and tell the room lead in person. The health status selection on the report is not
retained, so always use the note. See *How to create and save a daily report as a
draft*.

**How do I record a nap properly?**
Always fill in the **From** and **To** times — those are what the parent sees. Add any
detail about how the nap went, such as a short or missed nap, in the note to parents.
See *How to create and save a daily report as a draft*.

**The mood I picked reads as "Good".**
**Good** and **Okay** read back the same way, and an untouched report also publishes as
**Good**. If the mood matters, describe it in the **Note to parents** box. See *How to
create and save a daily report as a draft*.

**Where do I record medication given today?**
Use the free-text **Medicine** box in the **Daily Care** section, and tell the room lead
in person. See *How to create and save a daily report as a draft*.

**Can I change a report after publishing?**
Yes — reports are not locked. Edit the teacher note with the pencil icon or re-open the
report. See *How to edit the teacher note on a report already sent*. Editing does not
re-send the email, so send it again if the parent needs to see the change.

**Can I unpublish a report?**
Press **Save Draft** after publishing and it goes back to draft. See *How to publish a
daily report*.

**What does "Report not created" mean?**
No report has been written for that child on that date. Create one and the envelope
button becomes available. See *How to create and save a daily report as a draft*.

**What's the difference between Draft and Published?**
A **Draft** is saved but not marked finished; **Published** means finished. Neither one
emails the parent — only **Publish & Email** or the envelope does that. See *How to
publish a daily report*.

**How do I look at an old daily report?**
Change the date on **Daily Reports** and open the child's card. See *How to review a
daily report for a past date*.

**A child is missing from the daily reports list.**
Check that their registration covers that date and that the weekday is among their
enrolled days. See *How to register a child into a class*.

**Can I print a daily report?**
Email it to the parent instead, with **Publish & Email** or the envelope icon. See *How
to publish a daily report and email it to the parent*.

**My teacher note turned up in the wrong box.**
Always fill **Note to parents** first. If you only have behaviour text to record, put
something in the first box too, and the two stay where you expect. See *How to create
and save a daily report as a draft*.

**Which rows appear on a class's daily report?**
That is set per class, in the class's daily-report settings.

## Curriculum and assessment

**How do I set up a new room?**
Create a class. You will need an age group first. See *How to create an age group*.

**How do I add a group?**
If you mean a room, create a class. If you mean an age band, create an **Age Group**.
See *How to create an age group*.

**How do I create an age group?**
Open **Age Groups**, add one and choose its curriculum. See *How to create an age
group*. Always fill in the description, because other screens show that rather than the
code.

**Does the number of children on a class update itself?**
No — it is a capacity figure you type by hand. Children are enrolled from their own
record. See *How to register a child into a class*.

**How do I set up our curriculum?**
Open **Curriculum** and create it, then add learning areas, aspects and attainment
levels beneath it. See *How to create a curriculum*.

**What's the difference between a learning area and an aspect?**
A learning area is a top-level division of the curriculum; an aspect is a sub-division
inside it, and aspects hold the objectives. See *How to add a learning area* and *How
to add an aspect to a learning area*.

**What does "aspect" mean?**
A sub-area of a learning area, where the objectives live. The printed assessment calls
it a sub-domain. See *How to add an aspect to a learning area*.

**What does "attainment" mean?**
A level on your nursery's own grading scale for how securely a child has grasped an
objective. See *How to set up attainment levels*.

**What are the standard attainment levels?**
There is no built-in scale — whatever levels exist are the ones your nursery created.
Ask your lead rather than assuming. See *How to set up attainment levels*.

**The rating dropdown is empty and I can't save an observation.**
No attainment levels have been set up yet, and every selected objective needs a rating.
Create the levels first. See *How to set up attainment levels*.

**How do I record an observation?**
Open **Observations**, pick class, teacher, child, curriculum, learning area, aspect and
age group, then link at least one objective with a rating and save. See *How to record
an observation*.

**Where do I write down a wow moment I saw?**
That is an observation. See *How to record an observation*.

**What does "objective" mean?**
A single learning goal a child is assessed against, found by combining an aspect with an
age group. You pick them inside an observation. See *How to link objectives to an
observation*.

**How do I add a new objective?**
Objectives are read-only in the portal. Work with the ones already set up for your
curriculum, inside **Observations**. See *How to link objectives to an observation*.

**Can I edit an observation after saving?**
Yes, apart from **Class**, **Teacher** and **Child**, which are fixed once saved.
Ratings can be changed freely. See *How to view, edit or delete a saved observation*.

**How do I make an end-of-term report for a parent?**
Use **Assessment Report** — set the date range and pick one specific class. See *How to
generate an assessment report*.

**My assessment report is empty.**
No observations fall inside the date range you chose. Record observations first, or
widen the range. See *How to generate an assessment report*.

**Why can't I run an assessment report for all classes?**
Generation needs one specific class, so change the dropdown from **All classes** every
time. See *How to generate an assessment report*.

**How do I print a child's progress report?**
Generate the assessment report, then use the per-child **Assessment Report** button or
the toolbar print option and choose **Save as PDF**. See *How to export or print an
assessment report*.

**What's the difference between an observation and an assessment?**
An observation is a single recorded moment tagged against objectives; the assessment
pages summarise all of them. See *How to record an observation* and *How to read the
Assessments summary page*.

**Where do I see how a class is progressing?**
On the **Assessments** summary page. See *How to read the Assessments summary page*.

**Observation notes I typed have gone.**
Put the detail that matters into the objective ratings and the fields that appear on the
saved observation, and keep your own written notes alongside as you always would. See
*How to record an observation*.

event*.

**The "Branches" field on an event is showing classes.**

## Admissions enquiries

**How do I add a new enquiry?**
Open **CRM Leads** and click **Add**, then fill in the required fields. See *How to add
a new enquiry*.

**Where do I put a lead?**
On **CRM Leads**. A lead, an enquiry, an applicant and a prospect are all the same
record here. See *How to add a new enquiry*.

**Where do I put an applicant?**
Applicants are enquiries — add them on **CRM Leads**. See *How to add a new enquiry*.

**How do I record a prospect who called about a place?**
Add them as an enquiry on **CRM Leads**, filling the form top to bottom. See *How to
add a new enquiry*.

**Where's the waiting list?**
The **CRM Leads** list is your pipeline. The dashboard's **Waiting List** figure counts
the enquiries at the admission stage. See *How to search, sort and filter the enquiries
list*.

**I can't save a new enquiry.**
The referral source and record source are both required on every enquiry, and they must
already exist. Ask an administrator to add the entry you need. See *How to add a
referral or a communication source*.

**A new enquiry already shows consent ticked and SEN set to Yes.**
Those are the form's starting values. Set consent and the SEN question deliberately for
every family as part of your admissions script, rather than leaving them as they are.
See *How to add a new enquiry*.

**How do I book a tour?**
Set the enquiry's status to **Book a Tour** and give the follow-up task a reminder date.
See *How to book and record a nursery tour*. The status and its task are the tour
record.

**Where do I see the tours booked this week?**
Filter or scan the **CRM Leads** list for enquiries at **Book a Tour** and check the
task dates. See *How to search, sort and filter the enquiries list*.

**How do I change an enquiry's status?**
Click the status badge, pick a new one and set the reminder date. See *How to change an
enquiry's status*. Every status change creates a task.

**I chose "Tour Taken" and it now says KYC.**
Those two names refer to the same stage, and different screens use different wording.
Read the badge text and agree with your team which name you use internally. See *How to
change an enquiry's status*.

**What does KYC mean?**
It is the information-gathering and verification stage on an enquiry. The status menu
also calls it **Tour Taken**, so check which wording your team uses. See *How to change
an enquiry's status*.

**What does "Book a Tour" mean?**
A nursery visit is being arranged and has not happened yet. The dashboard shows the
same stage as **Toured**, so go by the badge on the enquiry. See *How to change an
enquiry's status*.

**What does "Admission" mean on an enquiry?**
The family is moving into the application stage. The dashboard calls the same stage
**Application** and **Waiting List**. See *How to change an enquiry's status*.

**What's the difference between Lost and Rejected?**
**Lost** means the family withdrew or went elsewhere; **Rejected** means the nursery
turned them down. See *How to mark an enquiry as lost or rejected*. Read the badge
text rather than the colour — only **Lost** is red.

**How do I mark an enquiry as lost?**
Click the status badge, choose **Lost**, set the reminder date and record why in the
admin comment. See *How to mark an enquiry as lost or rejected*.

**Where do I write why a family didn't join?**
In the **Admin Comment** on the move dialog, or as a note on the enquiry. See *How to
add a note to an enquiry*.

**How do I accept an application?**
Press **Approve** in the enquiry's detail header. See *How to record that an enquiry
has been accepted*.

**The Approve button is greyed out.**
The enquiry has already been accepted — the button reads **Approved** once it is spent.
Move the enquiry to another status from the badge menu if you need to re-do it. See
*How to change an enquiry's status*.

**What happens after I approve an enquiry?**
Nothing happens on its own. Create the child record yourself and carry on with
enrolment. See *How to move an accepted family into enrolment*.

**How do I turn an accepted enquiry into a child?**
By hand. Copy the details from the enquiry's detail panel and create the child from
scratch. See *How to move an accepted family into enrolment* and *How to add a new
child*.

**What's the difference between registration and enrolment?**
On an enquiry, **Registered** is the end of the admissions pipeline. Actual enrolment is
a **registration** on the child record — the link between a child, a class, a session,
a date range and weekdays. See *How to register a child into a class*.

**I can't set an enquiry to "Registered".**
Add a **task** on the enquiry and set the task's lead status to **Registered**. See
*How to create a follow-up task on an enquiry*.

**How do I set a follow-up reminder on an enquiry?**
Add a task with a date and a reminder date. See *How to create a follow-up task on an
enquiry*. Check your enquiries yourself on those dates — nothing prompts you.

**Does anything remind me on the reminder date?**
No. Build a habit of reviewing the **CRM Leads** list and its task dates as part of your
weekly routine. See *How to search, sort and filter the enquiries list*.

**How do I add a note about what we discussed with a family?**
Use **Notes** on the enquiry. See *How to add a note to an enquiry*. The date is set to
today for you.

**Where do I record how a family heard about us?**
In both the **Referral** and the **Communication Source** on the enquiry. See *How to
attach a referral or source to an enquiry*.

**How do I add a new referral source?**
Open **Referrals & Sources** and add it. See *How to add a referral or a communication
source*. Only an administrator can open that page.

**I can't open Referrals & Sources.**
That page is for administrators. Ask yours to add the entry you need. See *How to add a
referral or a communication source*.

**How do I email a prospective parent?**
Create a task on the enquiry, or move its status, and type the message there. See *How
to create a follow-up task on an enquiry*. Editing a task afterwards does not send
anything again.

**How do I change the wording emailed to enquiries?**
Edit the email template for that status. See *How to edit an admissions email template*.
Save each status before switching to another, and remember that editing a template
sends nothing by itself.

**How do I send a payment link to a family?**
Copy the link out of the composed message and send it from your own email. See *How to
edit an admissions email template*.

**How do I export the enquiries list?**
Use **Export to Excel** on the dashboard's CRM details view, which gives you a
spreadsheet file. See *How to export admissions figures*.

**The dashboard CRM numbers don't match the enquiries list.**
The dashboard uses different wording for the same stages. Trust the badge on the
enquiry row. See *How to search, sort and filter the enquiries list*.

**I edited an enquiry task and its attachments are no longer listed.**
Re-attach the file when you save the edit, and keep the original somewhere outside the
portal. See *How to create a follow-up task on an enquiry*.

## Invoices and payments

**How do I bill a parent?**
Create an invoice against the child. See *How to create an invoice*. You need at least
one service to exist first.

**How do I raise a bill for fees?**
A bill is an invoice here, raised against the child rather than the parent. See *How to
create an invoice*.

**Where do I charge tuition?**
On an invoice, picking the tuition **Service** on a line. See *How to create an
invoice*. The fee fills in from the service and you can overtype it.

**How do I mark an invoice as paid?**
Invoices carry no paid or unpaid status. Record the money as a receipt, then read the
family's position on **Balance by Class**. See *How to record a payment and create a
receipt* and *How to view outstanding balances by class*.

**Where do I see if a bill is paid or unpaid?**
Use **Balance by Class**, which shows each child's billed total, received total and the
difference. See *How to view outstanding balances by class*.

**How do I link a payment to an invoice?**
Receipts are attached to a **child** rather than to a single invoice. Write the invoice
reference into the receipt's payment detail text so a colleague can match them by eye.
See *How to record a payment and create a receipt*.

**How do I record that a parent paid?**
Create a receipt for that child with at least one payment line. See *How to record a
payment and create a receipt*. Remember to click **Add Line** — the payment table
starts empty.

**Where's the money stuff?**
Money coming in is the **Account** group — **Services**, **Receipts**, **Invoices**,
**Payment Modes** and **Balance by Class**. Money going out is the **Finance** group —
**Suppliers**, **Expenses**, **Expense Types**, **Payment Voucher** and **Purchases**.

**My receipt won't save.**
Check that the **Description** is filled in and that you have added at least one
complete payment line. See *How to record a payment and create a receipt*.

**The receipt form doesn't show a running total.**
Add the amounts up yourself before saving if the figure matters. The total appears on
the saved receipt. See *How to record a payment and create a receipt*.

**How do I print a receipt for a parent?**
Open the receipt and click **Print**, then choose **Save as PDF** or print it. See *How
to print or email a receipt*.

**How do I email a receipt?**
Open the receipt and click **Email**. See *How to print or email a receipt*. This sends
a properly formatted attachment and is the most reliable document route in the portal.

**How do I email an invoice to a parent?**
Open the invoice and click **Email**. See *How to print or email an invoice*. The child
needs a linked parent with an email address on their record.

**Who owes us money?**
**Balance by Class**. See *How to view outstanding balances by class*. It shows billed,
received and the difference for every child.

**Where do I see arrears?**
On **Balance by Class**. See *How to view outstanding balances by class*.

**A family shows a grey balance — do they owe us?**
Yes. Read the sign in front of the figure rather than the colour: a **+** means the
family still owes, and a **-** means they have paid more than they were billed. See
*How to view outstanding balances by class*.

**Why do plus and minus amounts look the same size?**
All three columns show the amount without a minus sign, so the direction is carried by
the **+**, **-** or **=** prefix. Read the prefix every time. See *How to view
outstanding balances by class*.

**Can I get a class total from Balance by Class?**
The class chips filter the list rather than producing a subtotal. Add the rows up
yourself, or copy them into a spreadsheet. See *How to view outstanding balances by
class*.

**Can I see one month only on Balance by Class?**
It runs from your very first record up to the optional **As of** cut-off, so you can ask
for "everything up to 31 March" rather than March alone. For a single month, compare
two cut-off dates. See *How to view outstanding balances by class*.

**How do I add a fee item to the price list?**
Add a **Service**, with its fee, cost, VAT percentage and type. See *How to add a
service*. Services must exist before any invoice line can be saved.

**Where do I set VAT on a fee?**
On the service — each carries its own default VAT percentage and a **Tax Exempt** flag.
See *How to add a service*. On an invoice line the VAT percentage is fixed.

**Can I change a price on one invoice without changing the price list?**
Yes — the line **Fee** is editable even though it fills in from the service. See *How to
create an invoice*.

**I changed a service's price and old invoices didn't change.**
That is intended. Each invoice line keeps the fee and VAT it was saved with, so your
history stays accurate. Edit an individual invoice if an old one genuinely needs
correcting. See *How to create an invoice*.

**How do I give a discount?**
Per line, as a percentage between 0 and 100. See *How to create an invoice*.

**How do I add cash, cheque or card as a payment option?**
Create them as **Payment Modes**. See *How to add a payment mode*. A payment mode must
exist before any receipt can be saved.

**Does recording a cheque mean the money has cleared?**
The portal treats a cheque as received the moment you record it. Hold off recording it
until it has cleared if your bookkeeping needs that. See *How to record a payment and
create a receipt*.

**Can I invoice a whole class at once?**
Invoices are created one child at a time. Set aside a billing session each month and
work down your class list. See *How to create an invoice*.

**Can I set up recurring monthly invoices?**
Each invoice is created by hand for each period. Put a repeating reminder in your own
diary for billing day. See *How to create an invoice*.

**Can I delete an invoice after the family has paid?**
You can, so be careful — finance records are not locked and there is no undo. Check
twice before deleting anything on **Invoices**. See *How to create an invoice*.

**Why does everything say AED?**
Amounts are labelled AED throughout. The figures themselves are correct — read the
label as "your nursery's currency".

**Can I change the currency?**
The label is fixed. Agree with your team that it means your own currency, and state the
currency plainly on anything you send outside the portal.

**Can I export the invoice list?**
Print or email one invoice at a time from the invoice itself. For a list, copy the
figures into a spreadsheet by hand. See *How to print or email an invoice*.

**A printed receipt has "INV#" in the header.**
The number itself is the correct receipt number. Ignore the label. See *How to print or
email a receipt*.

**How do I search or sort invoices and receipts?**
Use the search box and click a column heading to sort. See *How to search and sort
invoices or receipts*.

## Expenses and suppliers

**How do I add a vendor?**
Add a supplier. See *How to add a supplier*. A supplier is needed on every expense,
purchase and payment voucher.

**Where do I record that we spent money?**
As an expense, against a supplier and an expense type. See *How to record an expense*.

**How do I attach the receipt for a cost?**
Use the **Attachment** field on the expense. See *How to record an expense*. Files need
to be under 2 MB.

**My expense attachment won't upload.**
It is almost certainly over the 2 MB limit. Compress or rescan the document at a lower
resolution and try again. See *How to record an expense*.

**How do I set up cost categories?**
Create **Expense Types**. See *How to create an expense type*. An expense type must
exist before an expense can be saved.

**Where do I record a purchase order?**
On the **Purchases** page, line by line with VAT. See *How to raise a purchase*. A
purchase and an expense are separate records.

**My purchase lines all went blank.**
Set **Tax Exempt** before you start entering lines on a purchase. If you switch it
part-way, re-enter the lines. See *How to raise a purchase*.

**How do I pay a supplier?**
Record a payment voucher. See *How to create a payment voucher*.

**What is a payment voucher?**
The record of money leaving your bank to a supplier, split across payment lines. See
*How to create a payment voucher*. A parent payment is a receipt, not a voucher.

**How do I approve an expense before it is paid?**
Agree approvals in your usual way outside the portal, then record the expense once
approved. Records are live the moment they are saved. See *How to record an expense*.

**A supplier has stopped trading — how do I hide them?**
Leave them in place and simply stop selecting them, which keeps your history intact. If
you need the list tidy, rename them with a prefix such as "ZZ" so they sort to the
bottom. See *How to add a supplier*.

**How do I export the expenses table?**
Use the **Copy**, **PDF**, **Print** and **CSV** buttons above the table. See *How to
export a finance table*.

**My export contains more rows than I filtered to.**
The finance exports give you the full table. Download it and filter in your spreadsheet
instead. See *How to export a finance table*.

**My downloaded file has no date in its name.**
Rename it with the date as soon as it downloads, so a second download does not sit
beside it as a copy. See *How to export a finance table*.

**Is there a profit figure anywhere?**
Not in the portal. Export your income and expense tables and work it out in a
spreadsheet. See *How to export a finance table*.

## Roles and access

**How do I give someone access to a page?**
Find the access name that controls it, tick it for their **role**, then have them sign
out and back in. See *How to give someone access to a page*.

**Who can change permissions?**
Your nursery's **Admin**. If **Roles & Access** is not in your sidebar, you are not
that role. See *How to change what a role can access*.

**I can't see Roles & Access.**
That page belongs to the Admin role. Ask your administrator for what you need. See *How
to give someone access to a page*.

**How do I create a new role?**
On **Roles & Access**, click **Add role**, name it and save — then grant its modules.
See *How to create a role*. A new role starts with nothing ticked.

**Can I give one person extra access without changing everyone?**
Access is per role. Create a role for what that person needs, grant it, and reassign
them on the **Staff** page. See *How to create a role*.

**I ticked the box but they still can't see the page.**
Access is read when the portal loads, so they need to sign out and back in. See *How to
give someone access to a page*.

**What are the roles?**
Principal, Registrar, Teacher, Assistant, Accountant, Admin and Guest, plus an unassigned
state. See *How to find out what your role is*. What each one can actually open depends
on what your administrator ticked.

**What can a Principal do?**
Everything. The Principal sees every page in the portal. See *How to find out what your
role is*.

**What does a Registrar do?**
Front-desk work: enquiries and tours, child and parent records, and enrolling children
into classes. Their exact access is whatever your administrator granted. See *How to
find out what your role is*.

**Can a Teacher see invoices?**
Only if someone ticked **Invoices** for the Teacher role. Nothing is fixed by the role
name alone. See *How to change what a role can access*.

**What is a Guest account for?**
A deliberately limited visitor, auditor or inspector account. What it can open depends
on what was granted to it. See *How to find out what your role is*.

**My role badge shows a name I don't recognise.**
It shows whatever name your account was given, which may be a custom label such as "Vice
Principal". See *How to find out what your role is*.

**Why do teachers get the staff clock-in sheet too?**
One access setting — **Daily Report** — opens all four attendance and daily-report
pages together. See *How to change what a role can access*.

**Someone can see arrears when I only gave them Invoices.**
**Balance by Class** opens for anyone holding **Balance by Class**, **Invoices** or
**Receipts**. Bear that in mind when granting finance access. See *How to change what a
role can access*.

**Can I delete a role?**
Yes, but check the **Staff** page first to see who holds it. Anyone still assigned to a
deleted role will need reassigning before they can work. See *How to deactivate or
delete a role*.

**Deactivating a role didn't ask me to confirm.**
The status dot on a role card flips on one click. Click it deliberately, and prefer
editing a role's access over switching it off. See *How to deactivate or delete a role*.

**How do I see everything a role can open?**
Open **Roles & Access**, click **Manage access** on that role and read the ticked tiles.
See *How to change what a role can access*.

## Finding and exporting information

**How do I search a list?**

**How do I sort a table?**

**How do I show more rows at once?**
Use the **Show N entries** control where a page has one. See *How to change how many

**How do I move between pages of a list?**

**How do I filter a list by date?**
Use the **From** and **To** date controls where the page has them. See *How to filter a

**How do I export attendance?**

**How do I download a list as a spreadsheet?**

**Which pages have no export?**
**Balance by Class**, the invoice and receipt lists, the enquiries list, daily reports,
**Suppliers**, **Expense Types**, **Services** and **Payment Modes**. Copy what you need
into a spreadsheet, or print the page. See *How to print a page or a record*.

**How do I save something as a PDF?**
Use the page's **Print** or **PDF** button, then choose **Save as PDF** as the
destination in your browser's print dialog. See *How to print a page or a record*.

**I clicked Print and nothing happened.**
Your browser blocked the print window. Allow pop-ups for the portal and try again. See
*How to print a page or a record*.

**Will Arabic export properly?**
Yes. Spreadsheet files carry the marker Excel needs to open Arabic correctly, and print
windows keep the right-to-left direction.

**Why are my export headings in English?**
The admissions export keeps English column headings so the file stays consistent between
downloads. Translate them after export if you need to. See *How to export admissions
figures*.

**How do I copy a finance table quickly?**
Use the **Copy** button above the table and paste into your spreadsheet. See *How to
copy a finance table to your clipboard*.

**How do I read the Live Dashboard?**
It has three tabs — **Main**, **Finance** and **CRM**. See *How to read the Live
Dashboard*. It refreshes itself about once a minute.

**The dashboard numbers look wrong.**
Unmarked registers are the usual cause, since those children count as neither present
nor absent. Reload the page before acting on a figure that reads zero. See *How to read
the Live Dashboard*.

**Is the green "Live" badge telling me something?**
It is decoration and always reads **Live**. Reload the page if you want to be sure a
figure is current. See *How to read the Live Dashboard*.

**What does "Active Staff" on the dashboard mean?**
It counts employees whose record is active — who is on the payroll, not who clocked in
today. For attendance use **Staff Attendance**. See *How to clock a staff member in*.

**What does "Late" mean on the Finance tab?**
Billed minus collected for that month. It is not an overdue figure. For a genuine
arrears position use **Balance by Class**. See *How to view outstanding balances by
class*.

**Can I trust the "Days Late" pill?**
Read it as "days since we billed" rather than days overdue, and treat its colour as
decoration. Use **Balance by Class** for the real position. See *How to view outstanding
balances by class*.

**How do I look at an older month on the dashboard?**
Use the **Custom Date** control on the **Finance** tab — the month pills only go one
month back. See *How to change the dashboard's date range*. Your choice resets when you
switch tabs or reload.

**Can I filter the admissions statistics by date?**
The month arrows move the CRM calendar only; the cards, graphs and details table are
all-time figures. See *How to read the Live Dashboard*.

**The two admissions donut charts look the same.**
Read the numbered cards and the source and referral bar charts, which are the clearest
view of your pipeline. See *How to read the Live Dashboard*.

**Where are the analytics?**
The **Live Dashboard** is your overview. See *How to read the Live Dashboard*.

**Can I select several rows and act on them at once?**
Every action is one row at a time. Plan bulk work as a short focused session rather than
looking for a select-all. See *How to search a list*.

**My search finds nothing even though I can see the record.**
Search looks at the fields that page nominates — usually the name — and only within
rows already loaded. Search the name, or use the page's own filters. See *How to search
a list*.

**My filters reset when I switched from Children to Staff.**
Choose the person type first, then set your filters. See *How to view attendance for a
past date*.

## What the portal does and doesn't do

**Can I mark an invoice as paid?**
No — invoices carry no paid or unpaid status. Record the money as a receipt against the
child, then use **Balance by Class** to see what that family still owes. See *How to
record a payment and create a receipt* and *How to view outstanding balances by class*.

**Can I edit attendance for a past date?**
No — the attendance boards work on today only. Make completing the register part of the
morning and home-time routine, and use **Attendance Reports** to view past days
read-only. See *How to view attendance for a past date*.

**Can I reply to a message from a parent?**
No — **Messages** publishes outward to the parent app and has no inbox. Reply by phone
or email as you normally would, and publish a new post if the whole room needs to know.
See *How to send a message to one parent*.

**Can I tell whether a parent read a message?**
No — there are no read receipts anywhere. If something is important, follow it up by
phone. See *How to confirm a message went out*.

**Can I attach photos to a daily report?**
No — the daily report has no photo upload. Send photos to parents through the **Media**
tab on **Messages** instead, which is built for exactly that. See *How to send photos to
parents*.

**Can I record who collected a child?**
Not as a dedicated field. Type the collector's name into the **Notes** box in the
attendance **Edit** panel, and keep the child's **Pickup** tab current as your list of
who is authorised. See *How to check a child out at the end of the day*.

**Does an accepted enquiry become a child record automatically?**
No — you create the child record yourself. Copy the details from the enquiry's detail
panel and add the child, then register them into a class. See *How to move an accepted
family into enrolment*.

**Can I edit a health record?**
Not directly. Delete the record and add a corrected one straight away, in one sitting,
so the right information is always on the child's **Health** tab. See *How to record an
allergy, medical condition or special diet*.

**Can I invoice a whole class in one go?**
No — invoices are created one child at a time. Block out a billing session each month
and work down the class list. See *How to create an invoice*.

**Can I set up recurring invoices?**
No — each period's invoices are created by hand. Put a repeating reminder in your own
diary for billing day. See *How to create an invoice*.

**Can I change the currency?**
No — amounts are labelled AED throughout. The figures are correct, so read the label as
"your nursery's currency" and state your real currency on anything you send outside the
portal.

**Can I undo something?**
No — there is no undo, and deletes are immediate. Prefer setting records **Inactive**
wherever a status toggle exists, and check twice before deleting. See *How to deactivate
a child who has left*.

**Does the portal save as I type?**
No — every form needs an explicit **Save**. Finish each task in one sitting rather than
leaving a form part-filled.

**Does anything happen overnight?**
No — there are no overnight jobs, no automatic register close-off and no automatic
invoicing. Everything in the portal is there because a person entered it.

**Does the portal send emails automatically?**
No — every email is sent by a person clicking a button. There are three: a daily report
to a parent, a receipt or invoice to a parent, and an admissions message on an enquiry.

**Will I be reminded about anything?**
No — there are no reminders, alerts or notifications, including on an enquiry's reminder
date. Build your own routine of checking the pages that matter. See *How to search, sort
and filter the enquiries list*.

**Does the portal alert me about an allergy or a high temperature?**
No. An allergy shows as a red badge on the child's card, which staff need to look for.
For a temperature, write the reading in the **Note to parents** box and tell the room
lead in person.

**Is there an accident or incident log?**
Not in the portal. Record incidents in your nursery's own paper or digital log, note
anything clinical on the child's **Health** tab, and tell parents through the **Note to
parents** box or a phone call. See *How to record an allergy, medical condition or
special diet*.

**Where do parents' complaints go?**
Handle complaints through your nursery's own process. Use **Messages** for anything you
need to broadcast to families. See *How to message every parent in the nursery*.

**Can I see who changed a record?**
No — there is no change history. This is the strongest reason to give every member of
staff their own login rather than sharing one.

**Will the portal warn me before I delete something important?**
Usually not. Treat every delete as final, check what depends on the record first, and
set things **Inactive** where you can instead. See *How to deactivate a child who has
left*.

**Can I export Balance by Class?**
No — that page has no export or print. Export **Invoices** and **Receipts** figures into
a spreadsheet and reconcile there, or copy the rows you need. See *How to export a
finance table*.

**Can I export the enquiries list row by row?**
Not row by row. Use **Export to Excel** on the dashboard's CRM details view for the
summary figures. See *How to export admissions figures*.

**Is there a ratios calculator?**
No. Work your ratios out yourself against the classroom occupancy figures on the **Live
Dashboard**. See *How to read the Live Dashboard*.

**Is there a timesheet or hours-worked total?**
No — clock-in and clock-out times are stored but not totalled. Export the history from
**Attendance Reports** and add it up in a spreadsheet. See *How to export a list to a
spreadsheet*.

**Can I bulk-delete or bulk-edit rows?**
No — every action is one row at a time. There are no tick boxes and no select-all.

**Who do I contact if something isn't working?**
Your nursery's **Admin** for anything about access, and your Peekaboo provider for
everything else. See *Getting Unstuck* for what to gather before you contact them.
