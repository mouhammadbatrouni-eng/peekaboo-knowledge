# How To — Everyday Tasks

This is your step-by-step library for the Peekaboo portal. Each entry is short and
self-contained. Bold text is exactly what you will see on screen, so you can scan a
page and match it.

The portal is at **[https://peek.peek-a-boo.app](https://peek.peek-a-boo.app/login)**.
Each how-to below carries an **Open this page** link that takes you straight there. If
you have not used the portal yet today, you may be asked to sign in first.

A few things that apply everywhere:

- Pop-up confirmation messages appear in the **top-right corner of the screen** and fade
  after about four seconds. Green means saved, red means something needs your attention.
- The sidebar — the menu running down the left-hand edge of the screen in English, or the
  right-hand edge in Arabic — only lists the pages your role is allowed to open. Your menu
  may therefore look different from a colleague's. That is normal.
- Buttons for editing and deleting are small round icons on each row — an **amber
  pencil** to edit and a **red bin** to delete.
- **Nothing is saved until you press the save button.** It is usually called **Save**,
  **Update** or **Save Changes**, and it usually sits at the bottom of the form. If you
  leave the page without pressing it, your typing is lost.

### A few words used in these steps

- A **dropdown** is a short list that drops down when you click a box, so you can pick
  one option from it.
- A **toggle** is a small switch you click to turn a setting on or off.
- A **pill** or a **chip** is a small oval label, often coloured, showing a status or a
  name. Some can be clicked to filter a list.
- A **tab** is one of a row of headings across the top of a form or page. Clicking one
  shows a different set of fields, and switching between them keeps what you have typed.
- A **dialog** is a small window that opens on top of the page and must be dealt with
  before you carry on.
- A **CSV** is a plain spreadsheet file. It opens in Excel perfectly well.
- **VAT** is value added tax. **TRN** is your nursery's tax registration number.
- **SEN** means special educational needs.
- A **pop-up** is a window your browser opens on top of the current one. Printing uses
  one, so if nothing happens when you print, your browser has blocked it.
- The **clipboard** is your computer's temporary memory for copied text. Copy puts the
  rows there, and you paste them into a spreadsheet.

## 1. Getting started

### How to sign in to the portal
**Who this is for:** Everyone with a Peekaboo staff account.
**Before you start:** A username and password from your administrator. Your username is
not necessarily your email address.
**Open this page:** [Sign in](https://peek.peek-a-boo.app/login)
**Steps:**
1. Open the portal address in your browser. The **Login** screen appears.
2. Type your username into **Username**.
3. Type your password into **Password**.
4. Tick **Remember me** if you wish.
5. Complete the **"I'm not a robot"** security check. This step is compulsory.
6. Click **Sign In**. The button reads **"Signing in..."** while it works.
**You will know it worked when:** A green **"Signed in successfully"** message appears
and the **Live Dashboard** opens.
**Good to know:**
- The security check resets after every unsuccessful attempt, so tick it again before
  you retry.
- There is no eye icon to reveal the password as you type it, so type carefully.
- Closing the browser does not sign you out. On a shared reception computer, always use
  the **Logout** button at the end of your shift.
- The login screen is always in English. You can switch to Arabic once you are inside.

### How to sign out
**Who this is for:** Everyone.
**Before you start:** Nothing.
**Where:** the **Logout** button at the far end of the top bar — on the right-hand end in English, on the left-hand end in Arabic. You can do this from any page.
**Steps:**
1. Find **Logout** at the far end of the top bar — on the right in English, on the left
   in Arabic. It is outlined in red with a door icon.
2. Click **Logout**.
**You will know it worked when:** The button reads **"Logging out..."** and you are
returned to the Login screen.
**Good to know:**
- There is no "are you sure?" question. One click signs you out straight away, so save
  your work first.
- On a narrow phone screen only the icon shows, without the word.
- This is the only reliable way to end your session on a shared computer.

### How to reset a forgotten password
**Who this is for:** Everyone. You do not need to be signed in.
**Before you start:** Know your username. Your account needs a working email address
on file.
**Open this page:** [Sign in](https://peek.peek-a-boo.app/login)
**Steps:**
1. On the Login screen, click the small **Forgot?** link beside **Password**.
2. Type your username into **Username**.
3. Complete the **"I'm not a robot"** check — on this screen it sits *below* the button.
4. Click **Continue**.
5. Check your email for a 6-digit code and type it into **Email verification code**.
6. Click **Verify Email**.
7. The code carries over into **Authentication code**. Correct it here if you mistyped.
8. Type a new password of at least 6 characters into **New password**.
9. Type the same password again into **Confirm password**.
10. Click **Reset Password**.
**You will know it worked when:** A green **"Password reset successfully."** message
appears and you go straight into the portal.
**Good to know:**
- The code is confirmed at the final step rather than at **Verify Email**, so if the
  last step reports a problem, the most likely cause is a mistyped code. Use **Resend
  Code** to get a fresh one and start again from the code step.
- Do not refresh the browser part-way through — you will return to a blank first step.
- If the masked email shown ends in `@peekaboo.com`, treat it as a placeholder rather
  than your real address.

### How to change the portal language
**Who this is for:** Everyone.
**Before you start:** Nothing.
**Where:** the flag button in the top bar, near the right-hand end. You can do this from any page.
**Steps:**
1. In the top bar, find the button showing a flag and the current language — **English**
   or **العربية**.
2. Click it. A small dropdown opens.
3. Click **English** or **العربية**. The active one carries a small dot.
**You will know it worked when:** The whole portal changes language immediately. In
Arabic the layout mirrors: the sidebar moves to the right, text aligns right and arrows
flip direction.
**Good to know:**
- Your choice is saved for that browser on that device only. Choosing Arabic at
  reception will not change it on your own laptop.
- A few items stay in English in Arabic mode — the **Peekaboo** name, some month labels
  and a handful of status words. Anything not yet translated shows in English rather
  than appearing blank.
- Clicking outside the dropdown closes it without changing anything.

### How to switch between light and dark mode
**Who this is for:** Everyone, including on the Login screen.
**Before you start:** Nothing.
**Where:** the small square icon button in the top bar, between the flag button and **Logout**. You can do this from any page.
**Steps:**
1. Click the small square icon button in the top bar, between the language picker and
   **Logout**.
2. Alternatively, scroll to the bottom of the sidebar to the panel labelled **Theme**
   with the sub-text **"Light - Dark"** and click the toggle there. Both do the same job.
**You will know it worked when:** The page recolours instantly.
**Good to know:**
- The icon shows the mode you will switch *to*, not the one you are in. A **moon** means
  you are currently in light mode; a **sun** means you are in dark mode.
- Your choice is remembered in that browser for next time.
- If you have never chosen, the portal follows your computer's own light/dark setting.

### How to collapse or expand the sidebar
**Who this is for:** Everyone.
**Before you start:** Nothing.
**Where:** the small square panel button at the very start of the top bar — the far left-hand end in English, the far right-hand end in Arabic. You can do this from any page.
**Steps:**
1. Click the small square panel icon at the start of the top bar — far left in English,
   far right in Arabic.
2. Alternatively press **Ctrl+B**, or **Cmd+B** on a Mac, from anywhere in the portal.
**You will know it worked when:** On a computer the sidebar shrinks to a narrow strip of
icons. On a phone or narrow tablet it slides out as a panel over the page instead.
**Good to know:**
- Hover over an icon while collapsed to see its name.
- Your choice is remembered for about a week.
- On a phone, tapping any sidebar link closes the sidebar for you.

### How to find out what your role is
**Who this is for:** Everyone.
**Before you start:** A screen at least 1024 pixels wide — the role badge is hidden on
smaller screens.
**Where:** the top bar, just to the right of the filled **Peekaboo Care** badge. You can do this from any page.
**Steps:**
1. Look at the top bar. Beside the filled **Peekaboo Care** badge there is an outlined
   badge showing your role name, for example **Teacher** or **Accountant**.
2. To see what that role can reach, look at the sidebar. It lists only the pages you are
   permitted to open.
**You will know it worked when:** You can read your role name in the top bar.
**Good to know:**
- If the badge is missing, your browser window is too narrow. Widen it or use a larger
  screen.
- Whole groups disappear from the sidebar if you have access to nothing inside them, so
  two colleagues in different roles see very differently shaped menus.
- Access is set per role, never per person. To change what you can see, an administrator
  changes your role's access or moves you to a different role.

### How to understand your menu
**Who this is for:** Everyone, especially new starters.
**Before you start:** Nothing.
**Where:** the sidebar — the menu running down the left-hand edge of the screen in English, or down the right-hand edge in Arabic.
**Steps:**
1. Look down the sidebar. Pages are gathered into named groups.
2. Match what you want to do to its group:
   - **Center** — your nursery's own details, sessions and subscription.
   - **Operations** — the **Live Dashboard**, **Children Attendance**, **Staff
     Attendance**, **Daily Reports** and the **Calendar**.
   - **People** — **Children** and **Staff**.
   - **Communication** — **Messages**.
   - **Early Childhood** — **Classes**, **Age Groups**, **Curriculum**, **Learn Area**,
     **Attainment**, **Assessments**, **Observations** and **Assessment Report**.
   - **Finance** — money going out: **Suppliers**, **Expense Types**, **Expenses**,
     **Payment Voucher** and **Purchases**.
   - **Account** — money coming in: **Services**, **Invoices**, **Payment Modes**,
     **Receipts** and **Balance by Class**.
   - **Reports & Admin** — **Attendance Reports** and **Roles & Access**.
   - **Admissions CRM** — **CRM Leads** and **Referrals & Sources**.
3. Click a group to open it, then click the page you need.
**You will know it worked when:** The page opens in the main area to the side of the
sidebar.
**Good to know:**
- There is no search box that covers the whole portal. Each page has its own search, so
  go to the right page first and search there.
- The browser tab always reads "Peekaboo Portal" whichever page you are on. Read the
  heading at the top of the content area to confirm where you are.
- If you land on a page reading **"Not Found"**, you have most likely mistyped an address
  or followed an old bookmark. Click **Open App** to return to the Live Dashboard. Your
  session is untouched and you are still signed in.

### How to get help when a page says your role could not be determined
**Who this is for:** Everyone; an administrator completes the fix.
**Before you start:** Nothing.
**Open this page:** [Staff](https://peek.peek-a-boo.app/app/staff)
**Steps:**
1. If you see **"Your role could not be determined"**, note the small grey line beneath
   the message.
2. Send that line to your administrator.
3. Your administrator opens **Staff** from the sidebar, under **People**, finds you,
   clicks the amber pencil, chooses a value in **Role** and clicks **Update**.
4. Sign out and sign back in.
**You will know it worked when:** You reach the Live Dashboard normally.
**Good to know:**
- Signing out and back in before a role is assigned will not change anything, so contact
  your administrator first.
- This is a different message from **"Access denied"**, which means you do have a role
  but it does not include that particular page.

### How to change a staff member's password
**Who this is for:** Administrators and anyone who manages staff records.
**Before you start:** The staff member must already exist.
**Open this page:** [Staff](https://peek.peek-a-boo.app/app/staff)
**Steps:**
1. Open **Staff** from the sidebar, under **People**.
2. Find the person and click the **amber pencil** on their card or row.
3. On the **Details** tab, clear the **Password** field and type the new password. Click
   the eye icon to check what you typed.
4. Click **Update**.
**You will know it worked when:** A green **"Updated successfully"** message appears.
Give the new password to the staff member.
**Good to know:**
- Staff cannot change their own password from inside the portal. They can use the
  **Forgot?** link on the login screen, or ask you to set one here.
- Passwords are visible in the Password column of the Staff table view, so treat that
  screen as confidential and avoid leaving it open at reception.
- If the save reports a missing field, it names one at a time. Fill it in and click
  **Update** again.

## 2. Children and families

### How to add a new child
**Who this is for:** Registrars, principals and anyone who manages child records.
**Before you start:** Nothing for the basic record. To place the child into a class you
will also need an active class and an active session.
**Open this page:** [Children](https://peek.peek-a-boo.app/app/children)
**Steps:**
1. Open **Children** from the sidebar, under **People**.
2. Click **Add Child**. It is at the top right-hand corner of the page.
3. On the **Child Data** tab, type the child's full name into **Child Name**.
4. Pick the date of birth in **Birth Date**.
5. Optionally set **Gender**, **Nationality**, **First Language** and **Social Security**.
6. Choose the **Parent** from the dropdown (the short list that drops down when you click
   the box).
7. If the parent is not listed, click the small **[+]** button immediately beside the
   **Parent** box and add them first. They are then selected for you automatically.
8. Set the seven toggles (small on/off switches) to match the family's signed consent
   form: **Status**, **Take Pictures**, **Pic Nursery**, **Pic Media**, **Pic Social**,
   **Staff Child** and **Child SEN**.
9. Optionally fill **Notes** and **Medical Notes**.
10. On a wide screen, add a photo by clicking the upload box, or by clicking
    **Browse ...** beneath it.
11. Click **Save Child** at the bottom of the form.
**You will know it worked when:** A green **"Saved successfully"** message appears and
the child shows in the list as a card with a green **Active** pill.
**Good to know:**
- Only **Child Name** and **Birth Date** are required. **Child Picture** carries a red
  asterisk but a child saves perfectly well without a photo.
- Save the child before you add health records, pickup people or registrations. Those
  three tabs need a saved record first. After saving, reopen the child from the list to
  carry on.
- The photo box responds to clicking rather than dragging, and is only available on
  larger screens. Use a desktop computer if you need to add a picture.
- One parent is linked per child. Record the second parent inside the parent's own
  record, and anyone else who may collect the child on the **Pickup** tab.

### How to edit a child's details
**Who this is for:** Registrars, principals and anyone who manages child records.
**Before you start:** The child record exists.
**Open this page:** [Children](https://peek.peek-a-boo.app/app/children)
**Steps:**
1. Open **Children** from the sidebar, under **People**.
2. Find the child using the **"Search by name or ID…"** box or the dropdowns.
3. Click the child's card, or the **amber pencil** on the card.
4. Change what you need on the **Child Data** tab.
5. Click **Update Child**.
**You will know it worked when:** A green **"Updated successfully"** message appears and
you return to the list.
**Good to know:**
- Use **Cancel** rather than the browser Back button to return to the list, because the
  form opens in place of the list.
- **Update Child** saves the **Child Data** tab. The **Health**, **Pickup** and
  **Registrations** tabs save through their own **Save** buttons and are stored as soon
  as you save them, so **Cancel** does not undo those.
- Treat the age shown on a child's card as a rough guide and work from **Birth Date**
  when the exact age matters.

### How to search and filter the children list
**Who this is for:** Everyone who works with children's records.
**Before you start:** Nothing.
**Open this page:** [Children](https://peek.peek-a-boo.app/app/children)
**Steps:**
1. Open **Children** from the sidebar, under **People**.
2. Type into **"Search by name or ID…"**. It matches the child's name or their **Social
   Security** number and ignores capital letters.
3. Use **All Status** to show only **Active** or **Inactive** children.
4. Use **All Classes** to narrow to one classroom.
5. Use **All Age Groups** to narrow to one age band.
6. Click **Clear** to start again.
**You will know it worked when:** The cards narrow instantly and the count in the header
updates.
**Good to know:**
- **Clear** resets the search, class and age filters. Set **All Status** back by hand if
  you have changed it.
- If nothing matches you will see **"No children match the current filters."**
- This page shows cards rather than a sortable table, and the list is read on screen.

### How to deactivate a child who has left
**Who this is for:** Registrars and principals.
**Before you start:** The child record exists.
**Open this page:** [Children](https://peek.peek-a-boo.app/app/children)
**Steps:**
1. Open **Children** from the sidebar, under **People**, and open the child.
2. On the **Child Data** tab, switch the **Status** toggle **off**.
3. Click **Update Child**.
**You will know it worked when:** The card's pill turns red and reads **Inactive**.
**Good to know:**
- This is the recommended way to retire a child who has left. They stay in the system
  with all their history, and you can reactivate them if the family returns.
- Deactivated children drop out of the **Active** filter, so your day-to-day lists stay
  clean.

### How to delete a child record
**Who this is for:** Registrars and principals.
**Before you start:** Be certain — deletion is permanent.
**Open this page:** [Children](https://peek.peek-a-boo.app/app/children)
**Steps:**
1. Open **Children** from the sidebar, under **People**.
2. Click the **red bin** on the child's card.
3. A dialog headed **"Delete Child"** asks you to confirm.
4. Click **Yes**.
**You will know it worked when:** **"Deleted successfully"** appears and the child is
removed from the list.
**Good to know:**
- If you see **"Cannot delete this child because related records exist."**, the child
  still has linked information such as registrations, pickup people, health records,
  attendance history or invoices. Deactivating is the better choice in that case, and it
  keeps the history you may need later.
- Clicking **Cancel**, or clicking the dimmed background outside the box, abandons the
  deletion quietly. Nothing is removed.

### How to record an allergy, medical condition or special diet
**Who this is for:** Registrars, room leads and principals.
**Before you start:** The child record must already be saved.
**Open this page:** [Children](https://peek.peek-a-boo.app/app/children)
**Steps:**
1. Open **Children** from the sidebar, under **People**, and open the child.
2. Click the **Health** tab. The tabs run across the top of the child's record.
3. Click **Add Health**.
4. Choose the condition in the **Health Description** dropdown.
5. If the condition is not in that list, click **Add New Health**.
6. Type the condition into **Health Name**.
7. Choose a **Health Type** — **Allergy**, **Chronic**, **Skin**, **Neurological**,
   **Dietary**, **Respiratory** or **Other**.
8. Click **Save** to add it to the list, then choose it in **Health Description**.
9. In **Comment**, type the child-specific detail: severity, symptoms, the medication to
   give and exactly what staff must do.
10. Click **Save**.
**You will know it worked when:** A green **"Health added."** message appears, the row
shows in the table, and a red allergy warning appears on the child's card in the
Children list.
**Good to know:**
- This is the safety-critical route. Only a structured Health record raises the red
  allergy warning on the Children list, so record allergies here rather than only in
  **Medical Notes**.
- For a food intolerance or special diet, choose the **Dietary** type. There is no
  separate diet screen.
- **Comment** is the only place child-specific detail can go, so be explicit and write
  it as if for someone covering the room for the first time.
- To correct a health record, delete it with the red bin and add it again. Copy the
  **Comment** text somewhere first so the safety-critical wording is not retyped from
  memory.
- **Add New Health** adds the condition to the list used for every child, so keep the
  naming consistent across your nursery.
- It is worth also summarising the key points in **Medical Notes** on the **Child Data**
  tab and clicking **Update Child**, so it appears on the main record too.

### How to add an authorised pickup person
**Who this is for:** Registrars and room leads.
**Before you start:** The child record must already be saved.
**Open this page:** [Children](https://peek.peek-a-boo.app/app/children)
**Steps:**
1. Open **Children** from the sidebar, under **People**, and open the child.
2. Click the **Pickup** tab.
3. Click **Add Pickup**.
4. Type the person's full name into **Name**.
5. Choose the **Relation** — **Father**, **Mother**, **Grandfather**, **Grandmother**,
   **Uncle**, **Aunt**, **Sibling**, **Nanny** or **Other**.
6. Enter the **Phone** and **Email**. Both are optional, but a phone number is strongly
   advised.
7. Click **Save**.
**You will know it worked when:** A green **"Pickup added."** message appears and the
person shows in the table.
**Good to know:**
- Four things are stored: name, relation, phone and email. The list itself is the whole
  record, so keep it accurate and review it each term.
- Remove people promptly when authorisation is withdrawn — click the red bin and confirm
  **"Delete Pickup Person"**.
- Pickup entries can be edited. Click the amber pencil, change the fields and click
  **Update**.
- There is no limit on how many people you add.
- Check this list yourself at the door before releasing a child, and confirm identity in
  person. The attendance board records the time of collection rather than the name.

### How to add a parent or guardian
**Who this is for:** Registrars and principals.
**Before you start:** You must be inside a child's form.
**Open this page:** [Children](https://peek.peek-a-boo.app/app/children)
**Steps:**
1. Open a child, or start a new one. You must be on the **Child Data** tab.
2. Click the **[+]** button next to the **Parent** dropdown.
3. In the **Add New Parent** window, type their name into **Parent Name**.
4. Enter their email address into **Email 1**.
5. Optionally fill **Phone 1**, **Parent Country**, **Address**, **Phone 2**, **Email 2**
   and **Social Security**.
6. Check the login details. **User Name** is filled in for you. To get a different one,
   click the circular-arrow button beside the box, or type your own.
7. Replace the pre-filled **Password** with one of your own choosing.
8. Leave **Use Apps** on **Yes** to give the parent access to the parent app.
9. Set **Use Mail** to control whether they receive email notifications.
10. Leave **Parent Status** on **Active**.
11. Under **Spouse Contact Details**, record the second parent — **Spouse Name**, **Spouse
    Phone** and **Spouse Email**.
12. Under **Emergency Contact Details**, fill **Contact Name**, **Phone 1** and **Phone 2**.
13. Click **Save**. This closes the **Add New Parent** window.
14. Back on the main form, click **Save Child** or **Update Child** to attach the parent
    to the child.
**You will know it worked when:** A message confirms the parent was added, the parent is
selected in the **Parent** dropdown, and the child saves with them attached.
**Good to know:**
- The last step is essential. Creating the parent alone does not link them — you must save the
  child afterwards.
- Change the pre-filled password before saving. It is there for convenience rather than
  security.
- Only **Parent Name** and **Email 1** are required, but fill in the phone numbers and
  emergency contact while you have the family in front of you. Take care to get the
  details right at this point, as parent records are created here and cannot be edited
  afterwards from elsewhere in the portal.
- One parent is linked per child. The second parent goes in **Spouse Contact Details**,
  and anyone else who may collect the child goes on the **Pickup** tab.

### How to register a child into a class
**Who this is for:** Registrars and principals.
**Before you start:** An active class and an active session must exist. The child must be
saved, with their **Parent** already set.
**Open this page:** [Children](https://peek.peek-a-boo.app/app/children)
**Steps:**
1. Open **Children** from the sidebar, under **People**, and open the child.
2. Click the **Registrations** tab.
3. Click **Add Registration**.
4. Choose the **Class**. Only active classes are listed.
5. Set the **From / To Date** pair. Both are required.
6. Under **Days**, click each day the child attends. Use **Select all** to tick them all.
7. Choose the **Timing** — the session, shown for example as `Morning (08:00–12:00)`.
8. Leave **Status** on **Active**.
9. Click **Save**.
**You will know it worked when:** A green **"Registration added."** message appears and
the row shows with a green **Active** badge. The child now appears in attendance, daily
reports and observation lists for that class.
**Good to know:**
- Set the child's **Parent** and save the child before adding the registration. The
  parent is copied onto the registration, and this is what allows daily reports to be
  emailed to the family later.
- Check the **To** date falls after the **From** date before you save.
- The **Days** you tick genuinely matter. A child enrolled only Monday and Wednesday will
  not appear in Daily Reports on a Tuesday, which is exactly as intended.
- A child may hold several registrations, which is useful when they change room mid-year.
- This is where a child is enrolled into a class. The **No. of Children** figure on the
  Classes page is a capacity you type yourself rather than a live count.
- If the **Class** list is empty, create an active class first. If the **Timing** list is
  empty, create an active session on **My Center**.

## 3. Staff

### How to add a staff member and create their portal login
**Who this is for:** Administrators and principals.
**Before you start:** The roles you want to assign must already exist.
**Open this page:** [Staff](https://peek.peek-a-boo.app/app/staff)
**Steps:**
1. Open **Staff** from the sidebar, under **People**.
2. Click **Add Staff**.
3. On the **Details** tab, complete the seven required fields: **Employee Name**,
   **Nationality**, **DOB**, **Phone 1**, **Email 1**, **User Name** and **Role**.
4. Choose the **Role** first.
5. Then click the circular-arrow button next to **User Name**, so the username it
   generates reflects that role.
6. Replace the default **Password** with a real one. Click the eye icon beside the box to
   check what you typed.
7. Set **Gender**, **Employee Status**, **Employee Code**, **Phone 2**, **Email 2** and
   **Address** as needed.
8. Switch to the **More...** tab, at the top of the form.
9. On that tab, add **Emergency Contact Name**, **Emergency Contact Number**,
   **Capabilities**, **Joining Date**, **Employee Last Day**, **Employee Note**, **Social
   Security**, **Degree** and **Diploma**. All are optional.
10. Click **Save** — the same button is available from either tab.
11. Give the new member their **User Name** and **Password**.
**You will know it worked when:** A green **"Added successfully"** message appears and
the person shows in the list with a green **Active** pill. They can sign in straight away.
**Good to know:**
- Adding a staff member is how you create their portal login. There is no separate
  user-creation screen.
- The **Role** you pick decides what they can see in the portal.

## 5. Daily reports for parents

> **Recording illness, injury or temperature.** Write anything clinical or
> operational — a temperature reading, a bump, a child who seemed unwell — into the
> **Note to parents** box on the report, and tell the room lead in person before you go
> home. The mood and meal selections are a summary of the day for the family; the
> **Note to parents** box is where the important detail belongs, and it is the part
> parents read first. For an ongoing condition, allergy or medication, record it on the
> child's **Health** tab in **Children** so it raises a warning on their card.

### How to create and save a daily report as a draft
**Who this is for:** Teachers, room leads and assistants.
**Before you start:** The child must be registered into a class, with today among their
enrolled days.
**Open this page:** [Daily Reports](https://peek.peek-a-boo.app/app/daily-reports)
**Steps:**
1. Open **Daily Reports** from the sidebar, under **Operations**.
2. Click **New Report**. It is at the top right-hand corner of the page.
3. Under **Select Class**, click the classroom pill.
4. In the left-hand child list, click the child's name.
5. Fill in the sections that appear — all are optional:
   - **Today I Was** — click **Great**, **Good**, **Okay**, **Unsettled** or **Upset**.
   - **Meals & Drinks** — for each meal click **All**, **Most**, **Some** or **None**,
     then set the time and type a comment. For **Milk**, type the quantity into the **ML
     amount** box.
   - **Nap Time** — click **Slept Well**, **Brief Nap**, **No Nap** or **N/A**, and fill
     in the **From** and **To** times.
   - **Daily Care** — type free text into **Water**, **Juice**, **Medicine** and
     **Hygiene**.
   - **Items Needed** — click **Yes** or **No** for **Diapers**, **Clothes**, **Wipes**
     and **Cream**.
   - **Teacher Note** — write the **Note to parents** first, then **Activities &
     behaviour**.
6. Click **Save Draft**.
**You will know it worked when:** **"Daily report saved"** appears, a green line reads
**"Report saved for {child name}"**, and a green tick appears beside the child's name in
the list.
**Good to know:**
- Write the **Note to parents** before **Activities & behaviour**. That box is the heart
  of the report and the one parents read first.
- Record any illness, injury or temperature reading in the **Note to parents** box, and
  tell the room lead in person. That is the correct procedure for anything a parent or
  your colleagues must know about.
- Choose a mood deliberately for every report. A report saved without one shows as
  **Good**, so pick the one that matches the day.
- For nap, **Slept Well** and **N/A** are the two selections that carry through most
  clearly. Add the detail — how long, how settled — into the nap times and the **Note to
  parents** box.
- Write refused meals into the meal comment box as well as clicking **None**, so the
  family sees it in words.
- Type the milk quantity into the **ML amount** box rather than relying on the quick
  buttons beside it.
- Photos and videos are sent to parents from **Messages** rather than attached here.
- A new report is written against today's date, so write each day's report on the day.
- If a child you have just saved still shows "Report not created", reload the page.

> **How money in fits together.** Set up a **Service**, raise an **Invoice** for a child,

### How to add a service

### How to create an invoice
**Who this is for:** Accountants, registrars and principals.
**Before you start:** The child must exist, and at least one service must be configured.
**Open this page:** [Invoices](https://peek.peek-a-boo.app/app/invoices)
**Steps:**
1. Open **Invoices** from the sidebar, under **Account**.

   Invoice** form opens.
3. Set the **Date**.
4. Choose the **Type**.
5. Choose the **Child** being billed.
6. Leave **Tax Exempt** on **No** unless this invoice carries no VAT.
7. In the **Services** table, on the first row, choose a **Service**. **Fee** and **VAT
   (%)** fill in automatically.
8. Set **QTY**. Adjust **Fee** if charging a non-standard price. Enter **DISC (%)** if a
   discount applies, otherwise leave `0`.
9. **DISC Amnt**, **VAT Amount** and **Total** calculate themselves.

- Discounts are a percentage per line. Apply the same percentage to each line if you want
  a whole-invoice discount.
- Toggling **Tax Exempt** here keeps your lines and sets VAT to zero, so it is safe to
  change part-way through.

  register and raise them one by one.

> keep to it.

### How to add a supplier
**Who this is for:** Accountants and administrators.
**Before you start:** Nothing.
**Open this page:** [Suppliers](https://peek.peek-a-boo.app/app/suppliers)
**Steps:**

### How to record an expense
**Who this is for:** Accountants and administrators.
**Before you start:** An expense type and a supplier must exist. Have the receipt or
invoice file ready, at a maximum of 2 MB.
**Open this page:** [Expenses](https://peek.peek-a-boo.app/app/expenses)
**Steps:**

### How to create a payment voucher
**Who this is for:** Accountants and administrators.

**You will know it worked when:** The voucher appears in the table. Its **Amount** is the
sum of the payment rows, and its **Number** is generated for you.
**Good to know:**

- The bin is hidden when only one row remains, so there is always at least one line.

### How to raise a purchase
**Who this is for:** Accountants and administrators.
**Before you start:** A supplier and the services you are buying must exist.
**Open this page:** [Purchases](https://peek.peek-a-boo.app/app/purchases)
**Steps:**
1. Open **Purchases** from the sidebar, under **Finance**.
2. Click **+ Add Purchase**. The **Add New Purchase** form opens.
3. Set the **Date**.
4. Choose the **Type**.
5. Choose the **Supplier**.
6. Set **Tax Exempt** now, before entering any lines.
7. In the **Purchases** table, first row, choose a **Service**. **Fee** and **VAT (%)**
   fill in from the service record.
8. Enter **QTY** and adjust **Fee** if the price differs.
9. **VAT Amount** and **Total** calculate themselves.
10. For more items, click **Add Purchase** on the section bar, which adds a row. Remove a
    row with the red bin at its start.
11. Check the footer **Total VAT** and **Total**, which update as you type.
12. Click **Save**.
**You will know it worked when:** The purchase appears in the table with its
**Purchase#** and a VAT-inclusive **Amount**.
**Good to know:**
- Set **Tax Exempt** before entering any lines. Changing it afterwards clears them,
  because it switches to a different catalogue of services and sets VAT to zero.
- The **Fee** stays editable so you can record the price you actually paid. The VAT
  percentage comes from the service record.
- The calculation is: fee times quantity, plus VAT at the service's percentage. Purchases
  do not carry a discount column.
- If you see **"Purchase details failed to load"** while editing, click **Cancel** and
  reopen the purchase rather than saving.
- For a total across purchases, export to CSV and sum in a spreadsheet.
- Reload the page if a newly added supplier or service is missing from a dropdown.

### How to export a finance table
**Who this is for:** Accountants and principals.
**Before you start:** Nothing.
**Open this page:** [Expenses](https://peek.peek-a-boo.app/app/expenses)
**Steps:**
1. Open **Expenses**, **Purchases** or **Payment Voucher** from the sidebar, under
   **Finance**. These three pages carry the export toolbar.
2. Find the row of four small buttons above the table: **Copy**, **PDF**, **Print** and
   **CSV**.
3. Choose one:
   - **CSV** downloads a spreadsheet file immediately.
   - **Copy** puts the table on your clipboard as text you can paste straight into Excel.
   - **Print** opens a print-ready page in a new tab and launches the print dialog.
   - **PDF** opens the same print dialog.
**You will know it worked when:** The file downloads, the clipboard fills, or the print
dialog opens.
**Good to know:**
- To get a PDF, choose **"Save as PDF"** as the destination in your browser's print
  dialog.
- An export contains the full table rather than just your filtered view, so filter or sort
  the spreadsheet after downloading.
- Filenames are the same each time, so rename each download with a date as soon as it
  arrives — otherwise you end up with `purchases.csv` and `purchases (1).csv`.
- The files handle Arabic text and accented names correctly in Excel, and Arabic prints
  right-aligned.
- If nothing happens on **PDF** or **Print**, allow pop-ups for the portal and retry.
- For a single invoice or receipt, open the record and use its own **Print** button.

## 13. Finding, filtering and exporting information

### How to search a list
**Who this is for:** Everyone.
**Before you start:** Nothing.
**Where:** on whichever list page you are already using. Every list page has its own search box; there is no single search box for the whole portal.
**Steps:**
1. Open the page you want.
2. Find the search box. It usually sits at the top right-hand corner of the table, or
   just above the cards.
3. Type your search term. Most lists filter as you type.
4. On **Messages**, press Enter or click the round magnifier button to run the search.
**You will know it worked when:** The list narrows to matching rows.
**Good to know:**
- Each page searches its own nominated fields rather than every column, so search by the
  most obvious identifier — usually a name or a number.
- There is no single search box covering the whole portal. Go to the page that holds the
  information and search there.
- If a list looks empty, clear the search box to check whether it is genuinely empty or
  simply filtered. The count line beneath the table shows **"(filtered from N)"** when
  records are hidden.

### How to sort a table by a column
**Who this is for:** Everyone.
**Before you start:** You need a page with the standard table — **Payment Modes**,
**Services**, **Expense Types**, **Expenses**, **Payment Voucher**, **Purchases** or
**Suppliers**.
**Where:** on whichever table page you are already using. Click the heading at the top of a column.
**Steps:**
1. Open the page.
2. Click a column header. The first click sorts ascending.
3. Click the same header again to flip to descending.
**You will know it worked when:** The rows reorder under that column.
**Good to know:**
- To return to the original order, reload the page.
- Sorting keeps you on the page you are viewing, unlike searching, which returns you to
  page one.
- On **Invoices**, **Receipts** and **CRM Leads**, use the page's own **Sort** button
  instead. Invoices and Receipts sort by date, and CRM Leads by student name.
- Where a column header does not reorder the rows, use the page's search and filter
  controls to find what you need.

### How to change how many rows show
**Who this is for:** Everyone.
**Before you start:** You need a page with the standard table.
**Where:** on whichever table page you are already using. Look just above the table, on the left-hand side.
**Steps:**
1. Open the page.
2. Find the control reading **Show** [dropdown] **entries**.
3. Choose **10**, **25** or **50**.
**You will know it worked when:** The table shows that many rows and the count line
updates.
**Good to know:**
- Changing the page size returns you to page one.
- **Suppliers**, **Expense Types** and **Payment Modes** show every row on one page, so
  scroll rather than paging.
- On **Staff**, read the list in table view and use the search and role filters to narrow
  it.

### How to move between pages of a list
**Who this is for:** Everyone.
**Before you start:** A list long enough to be paged.
**Where:** on whichever list page you are already using. Look at the bottom right-hand corner of the table.
**Steps:**
1. Look at the bottom right of the table.
2. Click the left or right chevron buttons to step one page at a time, or click a numbered
   page button to jump.
3. Read the bottom-left line, which reads for example **"Showing 1 to 10 of 42 entries"**.
**You will know it worked when:** The rows change and the count line updates.
**Good to know:**
- On **Messages**, use the **<** and **>** buttons at the bottom of the history, which
  pages ten at a time.
- Searching first is usually quicker than paging through a long list.
- The count line tells you the full size of the list, which is useful before exporting.

### How to filter a list by date
**Who this is for:** Everyone.
**Before you start:** Nothing.
**Where:** on whichever page you need the dates for. The list in step 1 below tells you where the date control sits on each one.
**Steps:**
1. Open the page that holds the date control:
   - **Attendance Reports** — set **From** and **To**.
   - **Daily Reports** — set the date picker to the day you want.
   - **Messages** — set **From** and **To**, then press Enter or click the magnifier.
   - **CRM Leads** — click **Filter** and use **Date Range**.
   - **Balance by Class** — set the **As of** date.
   - **Live Dashboard**, Finance tab — click a month pill, or use **Custom Date** for an
     older month.
2. Read the results.
**You will know it worked when:** The list or grid updates to the period you chose.
**Good to know:**
- **Attendance Reports** covers up to about a year at a time. Run a long history in
  shorter blocks.
- **Balance by Class** counts everything up to the **As of** date. Compare two dates to
  see the movement in between.
- On **Invoices**, **Receipts**, **Expenses**, **Purchases** and **Payment Voucher**,
  export to CSV and filter by date in your spreadsheet.

### How to export a list to a spreadsheet
**Who this is for:** Accountants, principals and administrators.
**Before you start:** Nothing.
**Where:** on whichever page you want the figures from. The list in step 1 below names the pages that offer an export.
**Steps:**
1. Open a page that offers an export:
   - **Expenses**, **Purchases** or **Payment Voucher** — click **CSV** above the table.
   - **Attendance Reports** — click **CSV**.
   - **Assessment Report** — click **CSV** above the saved-reports table.
   - **Live Dashboard**, CRM tab, **Details** view — click **Export to Excel**.
2. The file downloads to your browser's downloads folder.
3. Open it in Excel or your spreadsheet program.
**You will know it worked when:** The file appears in your downloads and opens with your
data.
**Good to know:**
- These files are CSVs, which open in Excel perfectly well, including the one labelled
  **Export to Excel**.
- On the finance pages, an export contains the full table rather than just your filtered
  view. Filter the spreadsheet after downloading. On **Assessment Report**, the export
  follows the **Search** box.
- Rename each download with a date straight away, since filenames repeat.
- Arabic and accented names display correctly in Excel.
- For pages without an export — **Children**, **Staff**, **Suppliers**, **Services**,
  **Payment Modes**, **Expense Types**, **Balance by Class**, **Invoices** and
  **Receipts** — copy the rows from the screen into a spreadsheet, or use the **Copy**
  button where the page offers one.

### How to copy a finance table to your clipboard
**Who this is for:** Accountants and administrators.

### How to print a page or a record
**Who this is for:** Everyone.
**Before you start:** Allow pop-ups for the portal in your browser.
**Where:** on whichever page holds the thing you want to print. The list in step 1 below tells you which button to use on each one.
**Steps:**
1. Identify which route applies:
   - A finance table on **Expenses**, **Purchases** or **Payment Voucher** — use **Print**
     or **PDF** above the table.
   - An attendance report — use **Print / PDF**.
   - A single invoice or receipt — open the record and click **Print**.
   - An assessment report — use the toolbar **Print** for the table, or the per-row
     **Assessment Report** button for one child.
2. A new window opens with a printable layout and your browser's print dialog appears.
3. To save a PDF, change the **Destination** setting to **"Save as PDF"**.
4. Click **Save**.
**You will know it worked when:** The document prints, or saves as a PDF file.
**Good to know:**
- **"Save as PDF"** in your browser's print dialog is the final step for every **PDF** and
  **Print** button. The one document the portal sends as a ready-made PDF is a receipt
  emailed to a parent.
- If nothing happens, allow pop-ups for the portal and retry.
- Arabic printing is supported, with right-aligned text and correct column order.
- To give a parent a copy of a daily report, email it to them from the report list.

### How to read the Live Dashboard
**Who this is for:** Principals, room leads and administrators.
**Before you start:** Nothing.
**Open this page:** [Live Dashboard](https://peek.peek-a-boo.app/app)
**Steps:**
1. Open **Live Dashboard** from the sidebar, under **Operations**. It opens on the **Main
   Dashboard** tab.
2. Check the date under the title matches today.
3. On **Main Dashboard**, read **Children Present**, **Absent Today**, **Active Staff**,
   **Classroom Occupancy**, **Late Arrivals** and **Upcoming & Pinned**.
4. Click **Finance** for the current month's billed figure, the green **% Collected**
   badge and the **Late** amount.
5. Click **CRM** for the nine pipeline cards and the **Calendar**, **Graphs** and
   **Details** view pills.
**You will know it worked when:** You see the three tabs' figures, refreshed automatically
every 60 seconds.
**Good to know:**
- Mark every child's register each day for the present and absent figures to add up to
  your roll. Children who were never marked appear in neither total.
- Late children count as present.
- **Active Staff** counts who is on the payroll. For who clocked in today, use **Staff
  Attendance**.
- **Classroom Occupancy** compares enrolment with capacity, while the "{n} present" line
  under the room name is who turned up.
- If a figure reads zero unexpectedly, or a red message appears, reload the page before
  acting on it.
- The dashboard is a summary view. Open the relevant page from the sidebar to work with
  the underlying records.
- The page refreshes itself every minute. Reload the browser for an immediate update.
- If you work very early in the morning, check the date under the title before reading the
  attendance figures.

### How to change the dashboard's date range
**Who this is for:** Principals and accountants.
**Before you start:** Nothing.
**Open this page:** [Live Dashboard](https://peek.peek-a-boo.app/app)
**Steps (Finance month):**
1. Open the **Live Dashboard** and click the **Finance** tab.
2. The row of pills at the top opens on the current month.
3. Click any pill to switch month. The pills cover last month and the next seven.
4. To reach an older month, use the **Custom Date** control on the right of that row and
   pick any month and year.
**Steps (CRM calendar month):**
1. Click the **CRM** tab. Use **‹** and **›** to step months, or **Today** to jump back.
**You will know it worked when:** The Finance figures update for the chosen month, or the
CRM calendar moves.
**Good to know:**
- The Finance month returns to the current month when you switch tabs or reload, so note
  down any figures you need as you go.
- Two Finance figures are all-time by design: the footer **"Across all months … still
  unpaid"** line and the **Outstanding Balances** list.
- **Late** on the Finance tab means billed minus collected for that month. Check the
  invoice dates before treating an amount as overdue.
- The CRM month arrows move the calendar. The nine pipeline cards, the graphs and the
  details table are all-time figures.
- Use the nine pipeline cards for admissions counts, along with the **Source** and
  **Referrals** bar charts.
- The **Main Dashboard** tab always shows today.
