This guide is for anyone who is about to use Peekaboo for the first time. You do not need any technical background. Read it once from top to bottom, then keep it beside you for your first week.

## What Peekaboo is

Peekaboo is the staff system for running a nursery. It is the screen the people who run the setting sit in front of: the principal in the office, the registrar at the front desk, the teacher in the room, the accountant doing the books. One Peekaboo installation serves one nursery organisation, which may have more than one centre.

Think of it as your filing cabinet, register, curriculum folder, noticeboard and cash book combined into a single website. Attendance, daily reports to parents, curriculum and assessment, admissions enquiries, invoicing, staff records and centre details all live in the same place, and they all point at the same child record. That is the real benefit: you can answer "is this child up to date on fees, and did they attend last week?" without cross-referencing three notebooks.

Peekaboo runs in your web browser (the program you use to visit websites — Chrome, Safari,
Edge or Firefox). There is nothing to install. The address is
**[https://peek.peek-a-boo.app](https://peek.peek-a-boo.app/login)**. It works in **English** and **Arabic**, and it has a light and a dark colour scheme. One thing to know from the start: Peekaboo is the **staff** portal. Parents do not log in here. Parents use a separate app, and Peekaboo publishes to it — daily reports and messages you send reach families through that separate app, not through this one.

## What a normal day looks like in Peekaboo

Before you learn any single screen, it helps to picture the whole day. Here is the rhythm of a typical nursery day and the pages it passes through, in order.

### Morning — the register

The day starts on **Children Attendance**, under **Operations** in the sidebar. As children arrive, you mark each one present, and record who is absent, late or excused. If you are also responsible for the team, **Staff Attendance** clocks employees in the same way.

One thing to build into your habit: both attendance pages work on **today only**. There is no date picker on them. Mark the register as the morning happens, not at the end of the week. To look up a past date, you use **Attendance Reports** instead, which is a read-only history.

### Through the day — the daily report

**Daily Reports**, also under **Operations**, is the care record that goes home to the parent. Across the day you record mood, meals and drinks, nap times, milk, nappies and items the child needs, and you write the note to parents.

You can save it as a draft and keep adding to it. When it is finished you **Publish** it, and when you want the parent to receive it by email you use **Publish & Email**. Those are two different things, and the distinction matters — publishing marks the report as final in the portal, emailing is what actually sends it to the family.

### Through the day — observations

When a teacher notices a child doing something worth capturing — a first attempt at scissors, a moment of turn-taking — that goes into **Observations**, under **Early Childhood**. You name the child, describe what you saw, and tag it against the learning objectives it demonstrates, each at an attainment level. Over a term these observations build up, and at the end of the term **Assessment Report** turns them into the printable per-child summary that parents receive.

### Any time — messages to parents

**Messages**, under **Communication**, is the nursery's noticeboard to families. You publish announcements, newsletters, photos and videos to selected children, and those items appear on the parents' timeline in the parent app.

Messages is a **one-way broadcast**. There is no inbox and parents cannot reply to it. If a parent needs to reach you, that happens the way it always has — by phone, by email, or at the door.

### Any time — admissions follow-ups

**CRM Leads**, under **Admissions CRM**, is where a new family's enquiry lives. Somebody rings about a place in September; the registrar records the enquiry, books the tour, adds notes after the visit, and sets a follow-up task so nobody is forgotten. The lead moves through its statuses until the family either enrols or does not.

When a family is accepted, creating their actual child record is a separate step you do yourself on the **Children** page. Reaching the end of the lead pipeline does not create the child for you — plan for that handover.

### End of the day and end of the month — money

**Invoices**, under **Account**, bills a named child for one or more services. **Receipts** records the money that comes back in. **Balance by Class** shows what each child still owes, worked out as billed minus received.

Money going out lives under **Finance**: **Suppliers**, **Purchases**, **Expenses** and **Payment Voucher**.

### And underneath all of it

**Children** and **Staff**, under **People**, are the two rosters everything else refers to. **My Center**, under **Center**, holds your nursery's own details — name, address, phone numbers, logo and session times.

## Signing in

1. Open **[https://peek.peek-a-boo.app](https://peek.peek-a-boo.app/login)** in your browser. You land on the **Login** screen.
2. In the **Username** box, type your username. This is a **username**, not necessarily an email address — your administrator issues it to you.
3. In the **Password** box, type your password. There is no reveal button (an eye-shaped icon that shows the characters you typed) on this box, so type carefully.
4. Optionally tick **Remember me**.
5. Tick the **"I'm not a robot"** box. This is a security check that confirms a person is signing in rather than a computer, and it sits just below the **Password** box.
6. This step is compulsory — **Sign In** will not proceed without it.
7. Click **Sign In**. The button reads **"Signing in..."** while it works.

You will see a green **"Signed in successfully"** message and land on the **Live Dashboard**.

### If sign-in does not work

- **"Please complete the captcha"** means you skipped step 5. "Captcha" is the name for the **"I'm not a robot"** check. Tick the box and try again.
- A red message about your credentials means the username or password was not accepted. Check both carefully. **The security check resets after every failed attempt**, so tick it again before you retry.
- If the message mentions an incomplete response, simply try once more. If it keeps happening, contact whoever supports your portal.

### If you have forgotten your password

1. On the Login screen, click the small **Forgot?** link. It sits to the right of the word **Password**, just above the password box.
2. **Step 1 of 3 — Username.** Type your username into the **Username** box.
3. Tick the **"I'm not a robot"** check. On this screen it sits *below* the button rather than above it.
4. Click **Continue**.
5. **Step 2 of 3 — Verify.** Check your email inbox for a six-digit code, then type it into the **Email verification code** box.
6. Click **Verify Email**.
7. **Step 3 of 3 — Reset.** The code carries over into the **Authentication code** box by itself, so you do not need to retype it.
8. In **New password**, enter a password of **at least 6 characters**.
9. Type the same password again into **Confirm password**.
10. Click **Reset Password**.

You will see **"Password reset successfully."** and go straight into the portal — you are not sent back to the login form.

Two practical points. Do not refresh your browser part-way through, as that returns you to a blank step 1. And if the reset does not complete at the last step, the most likely reason is that the code was mistyped or has expired — go back and use **Resend Code** to get a fresh one.

If you cannot get into your account at all, your administrator can set a new password for you from the **Staff** page.

### Signing out — please read this one

**Closing the browser does not sign you out.** Your session stays open, so the next person to open the portal on that computer lands in your account with your access.

On any shared computer — reception, a staffroom laptop, the tablet in the room — always click **Logout** at the end of your turn. It is the red-outlined button at the far end of the top bar. There is no "are you sure?" prompt; one click and you are out.

## Finding your way around

Once you are signed in, every page sits inside the same frame: a **sidebar** down the side of the screen, and a **top bar** across the very top of the screen that stays in place as you scroll.

### The sidebar

The sidebar is your menu. It runs down the **left-hand edge** of the screen in English, and down the **right-hand edge** in Arabic. It is organised into groups, always in this order:

- **Center** — My Center, Packages, and (for the principal) Centers
- **Operations** — Live Dashboard, Children Attendance, Staff Attendance, Daily Reports, Calendar
- **People** — Children, Staff
- **Communication** — Messages
- **Early Childhood** — Classes, Curriculum, Age Groups, Attainment, Learn Area, Assessments, and indented beneath Assessments: Observations and Assessment Report
- **Finance** — Suppliers, Expenses, Expense Types, Payment Voucher, Purchases
- **Account** — Services, Receipts, Invoices, Payment Modes, Balance by Class
- **Reports & Admin** — Attendance Reports, Roles & Access
- **Admissions CRM** — CRM Leads, Referrals & Sources

Scroll to the very bottom of the sidebar and you will find a panel labelled **Theme** with the sub-text **Light - Dark**.

You can collapse the sidebar (shrink it so it takes less room) to a narrow strip of icons. Click the small square panel button at the very start of the top bar — the far left-hand end in English, the far right-hand end in Arabic. Alternatively, press **Ctrl+B** on a Windows computer, or **Cmd+B** on a Mac. When it is collapsed, rest your mouse pointer on any icon and its name appears. Your choice is remembered. On a phone or narrow tablet the sidebar slides out over the page instead, and closes again as soon as you tap a link.

### The top bar

Reading across from the logo end — the left-hand end in English, the right-hand end in Arabic:

- **The Peekaboo logo** — click it at any time to return to the **Live Dashboard**.
- **Chat** — a speech-bubble button. See the note below.
- **Two badges** (small oval labels) — a **Peekaboo Care** badge, which is simply branding, and next to it **a badge showing your role**, for example **Teacher** or **Accountant**. These appear on wider screens; if your window is narrow they are hidden.
- **The language picker** — a button showing a flag and the current language.
- **The light/dark toggle** — a small square icon button.
- **Logout** — outlined in red, at the far end of the top bar.

There is no profile menu, no avatar, no notifications bell and no search box covering the whole portal in the top bar. Each page shows its own heading in the large area below the top bar, which is where the page's own content appears.

### The single most important rule

**The sidebar only shows what your role is allowed to open.**

Anything your role cannot open is not greyed out — it is simply absent. And if your role has nothing at all inside a group, the whole group heading disappears too. A colleague in a different role will see a differently shaped menu from yours, with groups you do not have and without groups you do.

**This is normal and it is not a fault.** Your sidebar is an honest and complete list of the pages you are allowed to use. If you cannot see something in the menu, you are not meant to be using it from your account.

What each role can open is configured for your nursery, so the exact shape of your menu is a local decision. If you genuinely need a page that is not in your sidebar, the change is not one you can make yourself — ask your administrator, who can adjust it for your role. After they make a change, **sign out and sign back in** to pick it up.

If you ever type or bookmark an address you do not have access to, you will see a short "access denied" panel. That simply means the page belongs to another role. Your session is fine.

## Switching to Arabic

The language picker is in the **top bar**, near the far end beside **Logout**. It shows a flag and the current language — **🇺🇸 English** or **🇸🇦 العربية**.

1. Click the language button. A small dropdown (a short list that drops down below the button) opens.
2. Click **English** or **العربية**. The one currently in use is marked with a small dot.

The whole portal changes immediately. In Arabic the entire layout mirrors: **the sidebar moves to the right-hand edge of the screen**, text aligns to the right, and arrow icons flip direction. Everything works exactly as before; it is simply the other way round.

Two things to expect:

- **Your language choice is saved per browser and per device, not on your account.** Choosing Arabic on the reception computer does not change it on your own laptop. Set it once on each device you use.
- **The Login and Forgot Password screens have no language picker.** They appear in English until you have been inside the portal on that browser and chosen Arabic there.
- A few items stay in English in Arabic mode — the **Peekaboo** wordmark itself is deliberately left as it is, and some short labels and month names appear in English. Nothing is missing or broken; where a phrase has no Arabic wording yet, the English is shown rather than a blank.

## Light and dark mode

Peekaboo has two colour schemes. Pick whichever is easier on your eyes; it changes nothing about how the portal works.

There are two ways to switch, and they do exactly the same thing:

- Click the **small square icon button** in the top bar. It sits between the language picker and **Logout**.
- Or scroll right down to the bottom of the sidebar, to the panel labelled **Theme** / **Light - Dark**, and click the toggle (a small switch) there.

The icon shows the mode you will switch **to**, not the one you are in — so a **moon** means you are currently in light mode, and a **sun** means you are currently in dark mode.

Your choice is saved in that browser and restored next time. If you have never chosen, Peekaboo follows your computer's own light/dark setting. The toggle is also available on the Login screen before you sign in.

---

## A note about the chat button

The **Chat** button in the top bar opens a sample panel that is not yet connected, so nothing typed into it is sent or stored. Please do not use it to contact anyone.

The real way to communicate with families is the **Messages** page, under **Communication** in the sidebar.

## Your first 30 minutes

Work through these in order on your first day. Do them on the computer you will actually be using.

1. **Sign in.** Go to **[https://peek.peek-a-boo.app](https://peek.peek-a-boo.app/login)** and use the username and password your administrator gave you.
2. **Complete the security check and sign in.** Tick the **"I'm not a robot"** box, then click **Sign In**.
3. **Look at your role badge.** In the top bar, find the outlined badge next to **Peekaboo Care**. That is your role. Say it out loud — it explains everything else you are about to see.
4. **Read your sidebar from top to bottom.** Note which groups you have. This is the complete list of what you can open. Do not worry about what is not there.
5. **Set your language.** If you want Arabic, set it now with the language picker in the top bar. Remember to do the same on any other computer or tablet you use.
6. **Set light or dark mode.** Use the small square icon button in the top bar, or the **Theme** toggle at the very bottom of the sidebar.
7. **Try collapsing the sidebar.** Click the small square panel button at the very start of the top bar, or press **Ctrl+B** on Windows / **Cmd+B** on a Mac. Then expand it again.
8. **Open the Live Dashboard.** Click the Peekaboo logo at the start of the top bar. Have a look at the figures. Nothing here can be edited — it is a summary screen, and it is the one page that updates its own numbers as you watch.
9. **Open the first page of your own job.** For most people that is [Children Attendance](https://peek.peek-a-boo.app/app/attendance), [Daily Reports](https://peek.peek-a-boo.app/app/daily-reports), [CRM Leads](https://peek.peek-a-boo.app/app/crm/leads) or [Invoices](https://peek.peek-a-boo.app/app/invoices). Just look; do not enter anything yet.
10. **Find your role track.** Open *Training Tracks by Role* and read the track for your role from beginning to end.
11. **Practise signing out.** Click the red-outlined **Logout** button at the far end of the top bar.
12. **Sign back in again.** Get comfortable with this — on a shared computer it is the habit that matters most.
