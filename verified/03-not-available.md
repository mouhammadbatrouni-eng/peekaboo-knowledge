# What Peekaboo Does Not Do

Every system has a shape, and knowing where Peekaboo's edges are saves a lot of hunting for a button that was never there. Below is a plain list of things staff reasonably expect, which the portal does not do, each with the nearest thing that does work.

None of these are faults. They are simply the current shape of the product.

---

## Forms and requests

**There is no Forms module and no form builder.**
You cannot design your own form, publish it to parents or staff, or collect responses through the portal.
*Nearest thing:* for gathering information from families, the **Messages** page can send an **Announcement** or **Notification** carrying a **URL** and an **Attachment**, so a form hosted elsewhere can be shared that way.

**There is no staff requests feature.**
Staff cannot raise leave requests, holiday requests or any other kind of request for approval in the portal.
*Nearest thing:* **Staff Attendance** records a day as **On Leave** once the absence is known, and a staff record carries an **Employee Note**.

**There is no admission form link and no payment link.**
The portal does not generate a link you can send to a parent, and there is no button that emails one. An enquiry cannot be turned into a link for a parent to fill in or pay through.
*Nearest thing:* email the parent yourself, using the address recorded on their lead. Record what you sent, and when, as a **Note** on the lead so the team can see it.

---

## Pages that are not part of the portal

**There is no Incidents page.** You cannot log an accident or incident record.
*Nearest thing:* the **Daily Report** carries a temperature reading with a **Normal** or **Elevated** indication, plus **Activities & behaviour** and a **Note to parents**, which is where information about a child's day is recorded and shared.

**There is no Health page.** There is no place to manage medical records, allergies lists or health alerts as a page of their own.
*Nearest thing:* the **Children** page tracks how many children are **with allergies** and **with medical conditions**, an allergy indicator appears on the attendance board and in the daily report, and medical information lives on the individual child's record.

**There is no Schedule page.** There is no weekly timetable or shift planner.
*Nearest thing:* the **Calendar** page holds events, holidays and key dates by month, and **My Center** defines your center's daily sessions with a **From Time** and **To Time**.

**There is no Complaints page.** Complaints cannot be logged and tracked in the portal.
*Nearest thing:* there is no in-portal equivalent, so complaints need to be handled outside Peekaboo.

**There is no Classrooms page.** There is no separate page showing classroom occupancy and capacity.
*Nearest thing:* the **Classes** page under **Early Childhood** holds each classroom's age group, child count, teacher ratio and assigned teachers, and the **Main Dashboard** shows **Classroom Occupancy**.

**There is no Objectives page.** Objectives cannot be browsed or edited as their own list.
*Nearest thing:* objectives are selected against a child while recording an **Observation**, and each selected objective is given an attainment level there.

If somebody mentions one of these pages, they may have seen it in a demonstration or a different system. They are not part of what your center has.

## Invoices and receipts

**An invoice cannot be marked as paid.** There is no paid, unpaid or overdue status on an invoice, and no button to settle one.
*Nearest thing:* record the money in as a **Receipt**, then use **Balance by Class** to see each family's **Billed**, **Received** and **Balance**. The balance is what tells you whether a family has paid.

**Receipts are not linked to invoices.** A receipt is recorded against a child, not against a particular invoice, so you cannot see which invoice a payment settled or pull up a list of payments for one invoice.
*Nearest thing:* both sit against the child, so **Balance by Class** gives you the overall picture per family, and a receipt's **Description** and **Reference** are where a note about what the payment covers can be kept.

**There is no bulk invoicing.** You cannot raise invoices for a whole class or the whole nursery in one action. Each invoice is created for one child.

**There is no recurring or automatic invoicing.** Monthly fees do not raise themselves. Nothing generates an invoice on a schedule.
*Nearest thing:* the **Services** page keeps your fees and VAT rates set up in advance, so each invoice is quick to build from the price list.

**Currency is fixed.** Amounts show in the currency set on the center record, which is **AED** by default. You cannot bill one family in one currency and another in a different one, and there is no exchange rate handling.
*Nearest thing:* the **Currency Code** on the center record sets it for everything.

## Admissions

**A lead does not become a child record automatically.** Moving a lead all the way to **Registered** does not create a child in the **Children** page. There is no convert or enrol action that carries the details across.
*Nearest thing:* the child is added on the **Children** page as a separate piece of work, with the lead's details to hand. A lead can be flagged as **MOVED TO STUDENT AFFAIRS** to show it has been handed on.

**There is no online application form for parents.** Families cannot fill in their own details and have a lead appear.
*Nearest thing:* take the family's details however you normally do, by phone, by email or in person, and add the lead yourself on the **CRM Leads** page.

---

## Messages

**There are no read receipts.** You cannot tell whether a parent has opened or read something you sent.
*Nearest thing:* the **History** table records what was sent, whether it was **Published** and when, so you have a record of what went out even though you cannot see what was read.

**Parents cannot reply, and you cannot reply within a message.** Messages go one way, from the center out to families. There is no conversation thread.
*Nearest thing:* a message can carry contact details or a **URL** so families know how to respond by other means. Invoices, receipts and daily reports are emailed to the parent's own email address, so any reply arrives in your normal email.

**The chat panel is a demonstration.** The chat drawer you can open from the top of the screen shows sample conversations with placeholder names. It is not connected to your families and nothing typed into it reaches anybody.
*Nearest thing:* use the **Messages** page, which really does send.

---

## Automatic behaviour

**Nothing happens on its own.** Peekaboo does not run anything in the background. There is no overnight processing, no scheduled job, no automatic rollover from one day or month to the next.

**There are no notifications or alerts.** The portal does not notify you about anything — no badge for unread items, no alert when a fee falls overdue, no warning when a license is close to expiring.

**There are no reminders.** A **Task** against a CRM lead holds a **Set Reminder Date**, but the portal does not chase you on that date. The date is recorded for you to look at.

**Nothing is emailed automatically.** Emails go out when somebody chooses to send them — emailing an invoice or receipt to a parent, or using **Publish & Email** on a daily report. No email is triggered by a date or a status change on its own.

*Nearest thing for all of the above:* the **Live Dashboard** is the closest thing to an alert system. **Outstanding Fees**, **Pending Payments**, **Outstanding Balances** and **Days Late** surface what needs attention when you look at them, so the habit is to check the dashboard rather than wait to be told.

---

## Attendance

**Attendance is for today only.** The **Children Attendance** and **Staff Attendance** pages work on the current date. You cannot move them to another date to fill in a register you missed or to mark attendance in advance.
*Nearest thing:* **Attendance Reports** shows attendance history across any **From** and **To** date range for both children and staff, so looking back is well covered even though editing back is not.

---

## Other boundaries worth knowing

**There is no parent-facing login in this portal.** Peekaboo's portal is for center staff. Families receive information by email and through published daily reports rather than by signing in here.

**A child with related records cannot be deleted.** Once a child has history attached to them, removing the record is not possible.
*Nearest thing:* mark the child **Inactive** instead. They stop appearing in day-to-day use while their history stays intact.

**Role access is by page, not by record.** A role either can or cannot open a page. There is no setting that lets somebody see only their own class's children or only their own leads within a page they can open.

---

**How current is this?**

This describes the Peekaboo portal as at the current release. It covers what the portal does not do and what to use instead. It deliberately does not include step-by-step instructions for completing tasks — those are being verified separately and will follow.
