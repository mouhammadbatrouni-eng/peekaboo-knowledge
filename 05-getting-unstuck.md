# Getting Unstuck

When the portal does something you did not expect, find the closest heading below. Each
entry explains what is happening in plain terms, tells you exactly what to do, and says
who to ask if it is still not right.

Every heading is written the way you would say it out loud, so scan for the words you
would use.

Two instructions come up often below, so here is exactly what they mean.

**Reload the page** means asking your browser to fetch the page again. Press **F5** on a
Windows computer, or **Cmd+R** on a Mac. You can also click the circular-arrow button
near your browser's address bar.

**Sign out and back in** means clicking the red-outlined **Logout** button at the far end
of the top bar, then signing in again at
**[https://peek.peek-a-boo.app](https://peek.peek-a-boo.app/login)**. Closing the browser
is not the same thing and will not have the same effect.

---

### "I can't see a menu item my colleague has."

**What's happening:** Your sidebar lists exactly the pages your **role** is allowed to
open, and hides the rest. Your colleague holds a different role, so their menu is
different. A menu group with nothing in it disappears completely, heading and all,
which makes the two menus look further apart than they really are.

**What to do:**
1. Check your role. It is on the outlined badge in the top bar, just to the right of the
   filled **Peekaboo Care** badge.
2. If you cannot see that badge, your browser window is too narrow. Widen it, or use a
   desktop computer.
3. Ask your colleague to read out their own role badge.
4. If you genuinely need that page, ask your administrator to grant it to your role.
5. Sign out and sign back in afterwards. Access is read when the portal loads, so a
   change made while you are signed in does not appear until you do.

**Still stuck?** Tell your administrator the exact menu item you expect, the role on
your badge, and what you need the page for.

---

### "It says Access denied."

**What's happening:** You reached a page your role does not open — usually by typing an
address, following an old bookmark, or clicking a link a colleague sent. The menu never
disagrees with the rules, so anything not in your sidebar cannot be opened from
elsewhere.

**What to do:**
1. Reload the page once. Occasionally the portal could not confirm your access on the
   first try, and a reload settles it.
2. Navigate from the sidebar rather than from bookmarks.
3. If you need that page as part of your job, ask your administrator to grant it to
   your role, then sign out and back in.

**Still stuck?** Give your administrator the exact web address shown in the browser bar
and your role badge text. Being denied never signs you out — you can carry on working
from the menu straight away.

---

### "It says my role could not be determined."

**What's happening:** You are signed in, but your account has no role attached to it
yet, so the portal cannot work out what you may open. It is usually a newly created
account, or one whose role was removed.

**What to do:**
1. Ask your administrator to open the **Staff** page, find your record and set your
   **Role**.
2. Sign out and sign back in.

**Still stuck?** Tell your administrator your username and the small grey line of text
shown under the message — it tells them exactly what is missing.

---

### "I was signed out in the middle of my work."

**What's happening:** Your session ended and you were returned to the login screen.
Everyday network hiccups do not sign you out, so the usual explanations are that
someone clicked **Logout** on a shared machine, or browsing data was cleared.

**What to do:**
1. Sign in again and carry on.
2. Re-enter anything you had typed but not saved — nothing saves automatically.
3. On shared machines, give each member of staff their own account rather than passing
   one session around.

**Still stuck?** If it happens often on one machine, tell your Peekaboo provider which
computer and browser, and roughly how long you had been working before it happened.

---

### "I closed the browser but I was still signed in when I reopened it."

**What's happening:** That is how the portal works. Your session is kept in the browser
and closing the window does not end it. On a shared reception computer, the next person
to open the portal is still signed in as you.

**What to do:**
1. Always click **Logout** in the top bar at the end of your shift — this is the only
   reliable way to end your session.
2. Make explicit logging out a standing instruction for reception and staffroom
   computers.
3. If you find someone else's session open, log it out before you start.

**Still stuck?** Raise it with your principal as a safeguarding and data point rather
than a technical one. The **Remember me** tick box does not change this.

---

### "I saved it but the list still shows the old value."

**What's happening:** Some lists show their previous contents until they are reloaded.
Your work is saved.

**What to do:**
1. Reload the browser page.
2. If the old value comes back after the reload, open the record, re-enter the change
   and save again, watching for the green confirmation in the top-right corner.
3. Remember that nothing auto-saves — every form needs an explicit **Save**.

**Still stuck?** Tell your Peekaboo provider which page you were on and exactly which
field, and whether the green confirmation appeared.

---

### "It said it saved, but nothing changed."

**What's happening:** A few fields display their standard value when a record is
reloaded rather than the option you picked. Anything you typed into a free-text box is
the most reliable record.

**What to do:**
1. Reload and check which parts came back and which did not.
2. Put the information that matters into the record's main free-text box — on a daily
   report that is **Note to parents**.
3. For anything about a child's health or safety, also tell the room lead in person.

**Still stuck?** Give your Peekaboo provider the page name and the exact field, and say
what you selected and what came back.

---

### "A message with no detail appeared on screen."

**What's happening:** Something did not complete and the portal showed a short general
message rather than a specific one. It usually means the request did not reach the
server, or it came back without an explanation.

**What to do:**
1. Check your internet connection, then reload the page.
2. Try the same action once more. Most of these clear on a second attempt.
3. If it was a save, reload first and check whether the record went through before you
   re-enter it, so you do not create a duplicate.

**Still stuck?** Tell your Peekaboo provider which page you were on, exactly which
button you pressed, and the wording of the message.

---

### "A parent says they never got the daily report."

**What's happening:** **Publish** marks a report finished but does not send it.
Emailing is a separate step. This is the most common mix-up on the page and is easy to
put right.

**What to do:**
1. Open **Daily Reports** and set the date picker to that report's date.
2. Find the child's card in the left-hand list.
3. Click the **envelope icon** on the right-hand side of the card. That sends the report
   you already saved.
4. If the envelope is greyed out, no report exists for that date — create one first.
5. Check the child has a linked parent with an email address on their record. Without
   one, there is nowhere to send it.
6. In future, use **Publish & Email** to do both steps in one click.

**Still stuck?** Note the confirmation wording you saw and tell your Peekaboo provider,
along with the child's name and the date of the report.

---

### "I edited a report I had already emailed — does the parent see the change?"

**What's happening:** Editing a note does not send the report again. The parent still
has the version you sent.

**What to do:**
1. Make your edit and save it.
2. Click the **envelope icon** on the right-hand side of the child's card to send the
   updated report.
3. If the change is urgent or about the child's wellbeing, phone the parent as well.

**Still stuck?** Ask your room lead what your nursery's practice is for corrections
after a report has gone out.

---

### "An upload was refused."

**What's happening:** Some upload slots have a size or file-type limit, and the message
on screen names the file that was too big.

**What to do:**
1. Check the limit for where you are: photos on **Messages** need to be JPEG or PNG
   under 1 MB, videos up to 35 MB, and an expense attachment up to 2 MB.
2. Resize or compress the file, or convert a photo to JPEG or PNG. Phone photos are
   often far larger than 1 MB straight from the camera.
3. Try again with the smaller file.

**Still stuck?** Tell your Peekaboo provider the file size, the file type and which
upload slot you were using.

---

### "One file was too big and it cleared my whole selection."

**What's happening:** The size check runs across everything you picked at once, so a
single oversized file takes the rest of the selection with it.

**What to do:**
1. Check file sizes before you select, especially for photos.
2. Add files in small batches rather than one large selection, so a rejection costs you
   less.
3. Use the hint under the picker — you can add more images after your first selection,
   building the set up gradually.

**Still stuck?** If you regularly send large sets of photos, ask your Peekaboo provider
about the best workflow for your nursery.

---

### "I can't mark this invoice as paid."

**What's happening:** Invoices in Peekaboo do not carry a paid or unpaid status. Money
received is recorded separately, as a receipt against the child.

**What to do:**
1. Record the payment as a receipt on **Receipts**, choosing the child and adding at
   least one payment line.
2. Open **Balance by Class** to see that family's position — billed, received and the
   difference.
3. Write the invoice reference into the receipt's payment detail text so a colleague can
   match them up by eye later.

**Still stuck?** Ask your Peekaboo provider about reporting on outstanding fees. Do not
edit an invoice's description to say "PAID" — nothing reads it and it will appear on
the printed document.

---

### "The invoice didn't change after I recorded a payment."

**What's happening:** Receipts are recorded against the child rather than against a
single invoice, so an invoice looks the same before and after a payment. That is
expected.

**What to do:**
1. Use **Balance by Class** for the family's overall position — it is the figure to
   quote to a parent.
2. Note the invoice reference in the receipt's payment detail text so the link is
   readable by a person.
3. If a family pays part of a bill, record exactly what they paid; the balance will
   show the remainder.

**Still stuck?** Tell your Peekaboo provider which child and which invoice, and what you
expected to see.

---

### "My purchase lines have disappeared."

**What's happening:** Switching **Tax Exempt** part-way through a purchase changes which
services are available and clears the lines already entered.

**What to do:**
1. Set **Tax Exempt** first, before you enter any lines.
2. Re-enter the lines and save.
3. On an invoice the same toggle is safe to change at any time — it is purchases where
   the order matters.

**Still stuck?** Tell your Peekaboo provider how many lines you lost, so they understand
the cost of re-keying.

---

### "The colours on Balance by Class look the wrong way round."

**What's happening:** The colour is not the reliable guide here. The sign in front of
the figure is. A **+** means the family still owes you, and a **-** means they have paid
more than they were billed.

**What to do:**
1. Read the prefix character on every row, not the colour.
2. Cross-check the **Billed** and **Received** columns for that child.
3. Train your team to read the sign — it takes one briefing and removes the confusion
   for good.

**Still stuck?** If you are quoting a figure to a family, check both columns before you
speak, and ask your finance lead if the two do not tell the same story.

---

### "An enquiry stage has a different name on a different screen."

**What's happening:** Some admissions stages have two names — one on the enquiry's
status menu and another on the dashboard. They refer to the same stage.

**What to do:**
1. Go by the badge text on the enquiry row in **CRM Leads**. That is your working
   record.
2. Agree with your team which wording you use internally, and use it consistently in
   conversation.
3. When quoting figures to management, say which screen they came from.

**Still stuck?** Tell your Peekaboo provider which two names you saw and on which
screens, so the wording can be aligned.

---

### "The figures look out of date."

**What's happening:** The dashboard refreshes itself about once a minute and updates
quietly in place, so a change you just made may not have been picked up yet. The green
**Live** badge is decoration rather than a status.

**What to do:**
1. Switch to another window and back, which prompts a refresh.
2. Reload the page if you want to be certain.
3. If a figure reads zero, reload before acting on it.

**Still stuck?** If a figure stays wrong after a reload, tell your Peekaboo provider
which card on which tab, and what you expected.

---

### "I can't find a page in the menu."

**What's happening:** Either your role does not have it, or you are looking for a page
under a different name. Rooms are **Classes**, journals are **Daily Reports**, enquiries
are **CRM Leads**, and bills are **Invoices**.

**What to do:**
1. Check the names above and look again — most "missing" pages are simply named
   differently.
2. If it is genuinely not there, ask your administrator to grant it to your role, then
   sign out and back in.
3. On a narrow screen, open the sidebar with the toggle before deciding an item is
   missing.

**Still stuck?** Tell your administrator what you are trying to do rather than which
page you want — they can point you at the right one.

---

### "The page looks blank."

**What's happening:** The page did not finish loading. It is almost always a momentary
connection problem rather than anything to do with your data.

**What to do:**
1. Reload the browser page.
2. If it is still blank, go back to the dashboard from the sidebar and open the page
   again.
3. Check anything you were part-way through saving, and re-enter it if it did not go
   through.

**Still stuck?** Tell your Peekaboo provider which page, what you had clicked
immediately before, and whether a reload fixed it.

---

### "Part of the screen is still in English when I'm using Arabic."

**What's happening:** A few phrases are shown in their original English wording. The
layout still flips correctly right-to-left, and the screen works normally — it is just
those specific phrases.

**What to do:**
1. Carry on working; the meaning of the surrounding screen is unaffected.
2. Note the exact English phrase and which screen it is on.
3. Remember that your language choice is remembered per browser, so set it again on each
   machine you use.

**Still stuck?** Send your Peekaboo provider the exact phrase and a note of the page —
that is all they need to add the translation.

---

### "My browser keeps offering to translate our Arabic pages."

**What's happening:** The browser is guessing the page language and getting it wrong.
The portal's own Arabic is unaffected.

**What to do:**
1. Dismiss the prompt.
2. Choose the option to stop offering for this site, so it does not come back.

**Still stuck?** Mention it to your Peekaboo provider — it is cosmetic for most people
but worth knowing about if anyone uses a screen reader.

---

### "The search box doesn't seem to do anything."

**What's happening:** Search looks at the fields that page nominates — usually the name
— and only within the rows already loaded. A term sitting in another column will not be
found.

**What to do:**
1. Search on the **name**, which is what most pages match.
2. Use the page's own filters where it has them.
3. Check the footer under the table: if it says **"(filtered from N)"**, search is
   working and simply found nothing.

**Still stuck?** Tell your Peekaboo provider which page and which column you were trying
to search on.

---

### "A search found nothing and now the list looks empty."

**What's happening:** An empty result and an empty list look the same on screen. Your
records are still there.

**What to do:**
1. Clear the search box and the list returns.
2. Check the footer for the **"(filtered from N)"** note, which tells you records exist
   but are hidden.
3. Watch for typos — one wrong letter empties the list.

**Still stuck?** If clearing the search does not bring the list back, reload the page
and try once more before contacting anyone.

---

### "The list is showing every row instead of pages."

**What's happening:** Paging is switched on for some lists and not others, so on some
pages you get the whole table at once.

**What to do:**
1. Use the search box to narrow the list instead of paging through it.
2. Where a **Show N entries** control exists, the options are 10, 25 and 50.
3. Use your browser's own find function to jump to a name in a long list.

**Still stuck?** If a list is long enough to slow you down every day, tell your Peekaboo
provider which one.

---

### "I clicked PDF and it opened a print window."

**What's happening:** That is how the PDF buttons work. The print window is the route to
a PDF — you choose the destination yourself.

**What to do:**
1. In the print dialog, set the destination to **Save as PDF**.
2. Choose where to save it and click save.
3. If nothing opened at all, your browser blocked the window — allow pop-ups for the
   portal and try again.

**Still stuck?** Tell your Peekaboo provider which button on which page, and whether you
saw a blocked-pop-up icon in the address bar.

---

### "I clicked Print and absolutely nothing happened."

**What's happening:** Your browser blocked the new window before it could open. This
affects every print and PDF button in the portal.

**What to do:**
1. Look at the end of your browser's address bar, at the top of the browser window. A
   small blocked-pop-up icon appears there.
2. Click that icon and choose the option that allows pop-ups for this address.
3. Click the print or PDF button again.

**Still stuck?** Ask your administrator or IT support to allow pop-ups for the portal
across your nursery's computers once, and it will not recur.

---

### "My export has more rows than I filtered to."

**What's happening:** The finance exports give you the full table rather than the
filtered view. The file is complete rather than wrong.

**What to do:**
1. Download the file, then filter it in your spreadsheet.
2. Do not bother changing the page size first — that does not affect the export.
3. Rename the file with today's date as soon as it downloads, so repeat downloads do not
   pile up as copies.

**Still stuck?** Tell your Peekaboo provider which table you exported and what you
expected to get.

---

### "Nothing asked me to confirm before it deleted something."

**What's happening:** A few controls act immediately on one click — most notably the
status dot on a role card and delete on the calendar. There is no undo anywhere in the
portal.

**What to do:**
1. Re-create what was removed if you can. For a calendar event, add it again with the
   same details.
2. Before any delete, check what depends on the record — who holds a role, which
   enquiries use a referral.
3. Prefer setting records **Inactive** wherever a status toggle exists, which keeps the
   history.

**Still stuck?** Tell your administrator what was removed and when, so they can check
whether anyone else is affected.

---

### "I can't reply to a message from a parent."

**What's happening:** **Messages** publishes outward to the parent app. It is a
noticeboard rather than a mailbox, so there is nowhere for a reply to land.

**What to do:**
1. Reply by phone or email, exactly as you would for any other family contact.
2. If the whole room needs to know, publish a new post on **Messages**.
3. Use the chat button in the top bar for nothing — it does not reach parents. The
   **Messages** page is the real one.

**Still stuck?** Agree with your manager which channel families should use to reach you,
and tell them clearly at enrolment.

---

### "I can't record who collected a child."

**What's happening:** The check-out button stamps the time but does not ask who
collected the child. The written record goes in the notes.

**What to do:**
1. Click **Checkout** on the child's row.
2. Then click **Edit** on that same row.
3. Type the collector's name into the **Notes** box.
4. Keep the child's **Pickup** tab up to date as your list of who is authorised.
5. Keep doing your usual identity check at the door as well.

**Still stuck?** If your inspection framework requires a specific format, agree the
wording with your manager so every room records it the same way.

---

### "I can't edit attendance for a past date."

**What's happening:** The attendance boards work on today. Past days are there to read
on **Attendance Reports**, but not to change.

**What to do:**
1. Make completing the register part of the morning and home-time routine, so it is
   right before the day ends.
2. If you find an error later, note the correction in your nursery's own records and
   tell your room lead.
3. Use **Attendance Reports** to view what was recorded on a past date.

**Still stuck?** Tell your Peekaboo provider which date and which child, in case they
can help from their side.

---

### "A new enquiry already shows consent ticked."

**What's happening:** The enquiry form opens with some answers already selected,
including the consent boxes and the special educational needs question.

**What to do:**
1. Set every consent answer deliberately for each family, matching what they actually
   agreed.
2. Set the SEN question to **No** unless it genuinely applies — otherwise your figures
   will overstate it.
3. Make this an explicit step in your admissions script, so nobody relies on what the
   form opens with.

**Still stuck?** If enquiries were already created without checking, review them and
correct the consent answers. Tell your manager, since consent is a data-protection
matter.

---

### "Notes I typed seem to have gone."

**What's happening:** Some boxes show their standard value again when a record is
reloaded. The free-text boxes on a record are where information reliably stays.

**What to do:**
1. Reload and check which parts came back.
2. Re-enter anything missing into the record's main free-text box — on a daily report
   that is **Note to parents**, and on an attendance record it is **Notes**.
3. For anything about a child's wellbeing, tell the room lead in person as well.

**Still stuck?** Tell your Peekaboo provider the page and the exact box, and what you
had typed.

---

### "My teacher note appeared in the wrong box."

**What's happening:** The two boxes in the teacher note section are read back in order,
so filling only the second one moves that text up into the first.

**What to do:**
1. Always fill **Note to parents** first.
2. If you only have behaviour text to record, put something in the first box too, even
   a short line.
3. Reload once after saving to check it reads as you intended.

**Still stuck?** Tell your Peekaboo provider which report and what you had typed in each
box.

---

### "I pressed Save and nothing happened at all."

**What's happening:** A required field is empty. On the curriculum setup pages — the
curriculum itself, age groups, attainment and learning areas — a blank **name** stops
the save without a message.

**What to do:**
1. Check the **name** field first. That is the usual answer on those pages.
2. Scroll up the form, as some messages appear above the fold.
3. Fill in anything marked as required and save again, watching for the green
   confirmation.

**Still stuck?** Tell your Peekaboo provider which page and which fields you had filled
in.

---

### "I lost everything I'd typed when I clicked Cancel."

**What's happening:** Nothing auto-saves, and **Cancel** discards the form. Long forms
are safest completed in one sitting.

**What to do:**
1. Re-enter the details.
2. Click the save button — **Save**, **Update** or **Save Changes** — rather than
   **Cancel**.
3. For long forms such as a new enquiry, gather the information before you start.
4. If you are interrupted, save what you have — most records can be re-opened and
   completed later.

**Still stuck?** If you regularly lose work to interruptions, ask your manager whether
a quieter slot in the day would suit this task better.

---

### "I refreshed the page and lost my work."

**What's happening:** Work in progress lives on the screen until you save it. A refresh
starts the form again.

**What to do:**
1. Save before you leave a form, switch tabs or step away.
2. Finish each record in one sitting where you can.
3. If you were part-way through, reload and check whether an earlier save went through
   before you re-enter everything.

**Still stuck?** Note which form it was and tell your Peekaboo provider how far through
you had got.

---

### "I can't register a child — the Timing dropdown is empty."

**What's happening:** Registration needs an active session to choose from, and none
exists yet. This is the single most common thing that holds up a new registration.

**What to do:**
1. Ask whoever manages settings to add a **Session** on **My Center**.
2. Check there is also an active class to choose. A class needs an age group, and an age
   group needs a curriculum.
3. Make sure the child record itself has been saved at least once — the **Health**,
   **Pickup** and **Registrations** tabs open only after that.

**Still stuck?** The setup order is centre, then sessions and age groups, then
curriculum, then classes, then children. Ask your administrator to work through it in
that order.

---

### "I can't delete a child."

**What's happening:** Deleting is held back while the child still has registrations,
pickup people or health records attached. That is protecting their history.

**What to do:**
1. Set the child **Inactive** with the **Status** toggle instead. This is the right
   route for a child who has left, and nothing is lost.
2. If you genuinely must delete, remove the registrations, pickup people and health
   records first.
3. Remember inactive records still appear in list views — change the status filter to
   hide them.

**Still stuck?** Check with your manager before deleting any child record. Inactive is
almost always the answer.

---

### "I can't save an observation — it keeps asking about attainment."

**What's happening:** Every objective you link to an observation needs a rating, and the
rating list is empty until your nursery's attainment levels are set up.

**What to do:**
1. Ask whoever manages the curriculum to create your attainment levels.
2. Return to the observation, give each selected objective a rating, and save.
3. Check with your lead which scale your nursery uses — there is no built-in set.

**Still stuck?** Tell your administrator that the attainment list is empty; it is a
one-off setup task.

---

### "I can't generate an assessment report."

**What's happening:** The report needs one specific class and a valid date range.
Leaving the class dropdown on **All classes** stops it.

**What to do:**
1. Choose a single class from the dropdown.
2. Set both dates, with the **From** date before the **To** date.
3. If the report comes back empty, no observations fall in that range — widen it, or
   record the observations first.

**Still stuck?** Tell your Peekaboo provider the class, the dates and the exact message
you saw.

---

### "The attendance report totals don't match what I counted."

**What's happening:** Weekends are left out of every total, though they still appear as
tinted columns in the grid. Absent, excused and leave are also combined into one absence
figure.

**What to do:**
1. Recount excluding Saturday and Sunday.
2. Remember that public holidays count as ordinary working days, so allow for them.
3. Check for unmarked rows — those count as neither present nor absent.

**Still stuck?** When you quote these figures to anyone, say that weekends are excluded
and public holidays are not. That one sentence prevents most queries.

---

### "The dashboard attendance percentage looks too low."

**What's happening:** Children whose register was never marked are counted as neither
present nor absent, so the totals will not add up to your roll.

**What to do:**
1. Open **Children Attendance** and look for rows reading **Not Marked**.
2. Mark them and the figures settle.
3. Build a mid-morning check into the routine so the register is always complete.

**Still stuck?** If rows stay unmarked because nobody owns that room's register, agree
with your manager who marks it and by what time.

---

### "My filters reset when I switched between Children and Staff."

**What's happening:** Changing the person type on **Attendance Reports** clears the
group filter back to **All**. It is doing that on purpose.

**What to do:**
1. Choose the person type first.
2. Then set your dates and your group filter.
3. Export before you switch, if you have the view you want.

**Still stuck?** Nothing to report here — just set them in that order and it will behave
predictably.

---

### "Pressing Escape doesn't close the confirmation box."

**What's happening:** The confirmation box expects you to choose. Clicking **Cancel** is
the reliable way out.

**What to do:**
1. Click **Cancel** rather than pressing Escape.
2. Read the message before you click — on most pages this is your only checkpoint before
   a delete.

**Still stuck?** Nothing to report. If a dialog ever stays on screen after clicking
Cancel, reload the page.

---

### "There are two theme switches and I don't know which to use."

**What's happening:** There genuinely are two — one in the top bar, one in the sidebar
footer — and they do the same thing.

**What to do:**
1. Use either. They stay in sync.
2. Remember the setting is remembered per browser and per device, so set it on each
   machine you use.

**Still stuck?** Nothing to report; this one is purely a matter of preference.

---

### "I have several portal tabs open and can't tell them apart."

**What's happening:** Every page uses the same browser tab title, so open tabs look
identical.

**What to do:**
1. Work in one tab where you can.
2. If you need two, put them in a deliberate order and remember which is which, or pin
   the one you return to most.

**Still stuck?** Mention it to your Peekaboo provider as a small everyday annoyance
worth fixing.

### "Check-in times filled themselves in and I didn't type them."

**What's happening:** Marking a child **Present** or **Late** stamps the current clock
time for you. If a time is already there, it is kept rather than replaced.

**What to do:**
1. Use **Edit** on the child's row if you need a specific time.
2. Be careful marking a child **Absent** after checking them in — that clears both
   times.
3. If you clear a time by accident, re-mark them and set the correct time through
   **Edit** straight away.

**Still stuck?** Tell your room lead what the correct times were, so the day's records
are consistent.

---

### "The child I need isn't on the daily reports list."

**What's happening:** The list shows children whose registration covers that date and
whose enrolled weekdays include that day.

**What to do:**
1. Open the child's record and check the **Registrations** tab — the dates and the
   **Days** selection.
2. Correct the registration if the child has changed days or rooms.
3. Return to **Daily Reports** and the child appears.

**Still stuck?** Tell your administrator which child and which date, and what days they
should be attending.

---

### "I can't open Referrals & Sources, but I can't save an enquiry without one."

**What's happening:** That list is maintained by administrators, and every enquiry needs
a referral and a record source chosen from it.

**What to do:**
1. Ask your administrator to add the entry you need — for example a new school fair or
   a new website.
2. Meanwhile, choose the closest existing entry so the enquiry can be saved, and add a
   note explaining.
3. Update the enquiry once the correct entry exists.

**Still stuck?** Send your administrator a short list of all the sources your nursery
uses, so they can add them in one go.

---

### "A supplier has stopped trading and I want them off the list."

**What's happening:** Suppliers have no active or inactive switch, so the only removal
is deletion — and that risks your historical records.

**What to do:**
1. Leave the supplier in place and simply stop selecting them. This is the safe choice.
2. If the list is cluttered, rename them with a prefix such as "ZZ" so they sort to the
   bottom.
3. Avoid deleting anything a past expense or voucher refers to.

**Still stuck?** Ask your Peekaboo provider before deleting a supplier with history
behind it.

---

### "I deleted a role and now someone can't work."

**What's happening:** Anyone who held that role now has no role, so they see the message
about their role not being determined.

**What to do:**
1. Open the **Staff** page and reassign the affected people to a suitable role.
2. Ask them to sign out and back in.
3. Before deleting any role in future, check the **Staff** page for who holds it.

**Still stuck?** Tell your Peekaboo provider which role was removed and who is affected.

---

### "Access I granted hasn't appeared for my colleague."

**What's happening:** Access is read when the portal loads. A change made while someone
is signed in does not reach them until their next sign-in.

**What to do:**
1. Ask them to click **Logout** and sign back in.
2. Check you ticked the setting for the right **role**, not for a person — access is set
   per role.
3. Confirm they actually hold that role on their staff record.

**Still stuck?** Have them read out their role badge text to you, and compare it against
the role you edited.

---

### "Someone can see more than I intended."

**What's happening:** A few access settings open more than one page. **Daily Report**
opens all four attendance and daily-report pages, and **Balance by Class** opens for
anyone with invoices or receipts access.

**What to do:**
1. Open **Roles & Access**, choose the role and click **Manage access** to read exactly
   what is ticked.
2. Untick anything that role should not have, and have holders sign out and back in.
3. Where one person needs a different mix, create a role for that mix rather than
   loosening an existing one.

**Still stuck?** Ask your Peekaboo provider which pages each access setting covers before
you grant a new one.

---

### "I need a figure the portal doesn't show."

**What's happening:** Some totals are not calculated in the portal — hours worked, staff
ratios, profit, and per-class totals on **Balance by Class**.

**What to do:**
1. Export the underlying data where an export exists — attendance, expenses, purchases
   and payment vouchers all have one.
2. Work the figure out in your spreadsheet.
3. Where there is no export, copy the rows you need by hand and keep the working
   alongside them.

**Still stuck?** Tell your Peekaboo provider which figure you need regularly and what you
use it for.

---

### "I can't work out whether a family has paid."

**What's happening:** Invoices and receipts are separate records, so the answer comes
from the balance rather than from any single document.

**What to do:**
1. Open **Balance by Class** and find the child.
2. Read **Billed**, **Received** and the difference, checking the sign in front of the
   balance.
3. If you need the detail behind it, open that child's invoices and receipts side by
   side.

**Still stuck?** Ask your finance lead to confirm before you discuss a balance with a
family.

---

### "The enquiry statuses in my report don't match what my manager expected."

**What's happening:** The dashboard and the enquiry list use different wording for the
same stages, so two people can describe the same pipeline differently.

**What to do:**
1. Quote figures from one place and say which — the enquiry list badges are the working
   record.
2. Agree a shared vocabulary with your team and write it down.
3. When reporting, include the date you took the figures.

**Still stuck?** Share the two screens with your manager so you agree on the mapping
once.

---

### "I've been asked for something the portal doesn't record."

**What's happening:** Incidents, complaints, staff rotas and formal accident logs are
kept outside the portal.

**What to do:**
1. Use your nursery's own process and records for those.
2. Note anything clinical about a child on their **Health** tab.
3. Tell parents through the **Note to parents** box or a phone call, and tell the room
   lead in person.

**Still stuck?** Ask your manager where your nursery keeps each of these records, so
everyone uses the same place.

## When to contact support

Most things on this page you can settle yourself in a minute or two. When you do need
help, a good report gets you an answer far faster than a general one.

**Who to ask:**

- **Your nursery's Admin** for anything about access — a missing menu item, a denied
  page, a role that needs changing, a password reset.
- **Your Peekaboo provider** for everything else.

**Gather these five things before you contact anyone:**

1. **Which page you were on.** Give the name as it appears in the sidebar — for example
   **Daily Reports** or **Balance by Class**.
2. **What you clicked.** The exact button or link, using the wording on screen.
3. **What you expected to happen.**
4. **What actually happened.** Copy the wording of any message you saw, word for word,
   and take a photo or screenshot if you can.
5. **Your role.** Read it from the badge in the top bar on a wide screen.

**Also worth adding:**

- The date and rough time it happened.
- Whether it happens every time, or happened once.
- Whether reloading the page changed anything.
- The child, family, invoice or enquiry involved, by name or reference.
- Which browser and device you were using, and whether a colleague on a different
  machine sees the same thing.

**Before you send it, try these three:** reload the page, sign out and back in, and try
the same action once more. Between them they resolve a large share of everyday
problems — and if they do not, saying you tried them tells support a great deal.
