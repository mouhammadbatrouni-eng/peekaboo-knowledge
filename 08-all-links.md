# Every Page, One Click Away

Clicking any link below opens the portal at that page. If you have not used the portal yet
today, you may be asked to sign in first — sign in as usual and the page will open.

Two things worth knowing before you start. First, **your sidebar only lists the pages your
role is allowed to open**, so some links below belong to a colleague's job rather than
yours. If you open one that is not yours, you will see a short **Access denied** panel.
That is the account working exactly as intended, and your sign-in is unaffected — use the
back button, or the **Peekaboo logo** in the top bar, to return to the **Live Dashboard**.
Second, these links are worth saving as browser bookmarks (a saved shortcut to a page) for
the two or three pages you use every day.

## Operations — the pages the day runs on

### [Dashboard](https://peek.peek-a-boo.app/app)
The summary screen you land on after signing in. It shows today's headline numbers for
attendance, money and enquiries. Nothing here can be edited.
**Who normally uses it:** the principal and the office team, first thing each morning.

### [Children Attendance](https://peek.peek-a-boo.app/app/attendance)
The morning register for children — **Present**, **Absent**, **Late** or **Excused** — and
where you check a child out at home time. It works on today only.
**Who normally uses it:** teachers, room leads and assistants, and reception.

### [Staff Attendance](https://peek.peek-a-boo.app/app/staff-attendance)
The clock-in sheet for employees — **Present**, **Absent**, **Late** or **On Leave**. It
also works on today only.
**Who normally uses it:** room leads, the principal, and whoever runs the staff register.

### [Attendance Reports](https://peek.peek-a-boo.app/app/attendance-reports)
The attendance history for any past date range, for children or for staff. You read it
rather than change it.
**Who normally uses it:** the principal, the office, and anyone answering an inspection
or a parent's question about past attendance.

### [Daily Reports](https://peek.peek-a-boo.app/app/daily-reports)
The care record that goes home to the family — mood, meals, naps, nappies, items needed
and the **Note to parents**.
**Who normally uses it:** teachers, room leads and assistants, across the day.

### [Calendar](https://peek.peek-a-boo.app/app/calendar)
The nursery diary — holidays, trips, performances, meetings and key dates.
**Who normally uses it:** the principal, registrars and room leads.

## People — the two rosters

### [Children](https://peek.peek-a-boo.app/app/children)
Every child on your books, and behind each one their parent details, health records,
authorised pickup people and class registrations. Everything else in the portal points
back to this page.
**Who normally uses it:** registrars and principals to create and edit records; teachers
and assistants to look up allergies and pickup people.

### [Staff](https://peek.peek-a-boo.app/app/staff)
The employee register, and where a colleague's portal login and role are created.
**Who normally uses it:** the administrator and the principal.

## Communication

### [Messages](https://peek.peek-a-boo.app/app/messages)
The nursery's noticeboard to families. You publish announcements, newsletters, photos and
videos to selected children, and they reach parents in the separate parent app. It is
one-way; parents cannot reply here.
**Who normally uses it:** the office, the principal and room staff sharing photos.

## Your nursery's own settings

### [My Center](https://peek.peek-a-boo.app/app/my-center)
Your nursery's own details — name, address, phone numbers, email, logo, VAT details — and
the **Sessions** tab where your daily time slots are set. Anything about *our* nursery is
here.
**Who normally uses it:** the principal or the office administrator, a few times a year.

### [Centers](https://peek.peek-a-boo.app/app/centers)
The multi-nursery screen, where a brand-new nursery on the platform is set up. This is a
different page from **My Center**.
**Who normally uses it:** the principal alone, and rarely.

### [Packages](https://peek.peek-a-boo.app/app/packages)
Your nursery's own subscription to the Peekaboo software. This is not parent billing and
never appears on a family's invoice.
**Who normally uses it:** the principal or owner.

## Early Childhood — curriculum, observations and assessment

### [Classes](https://peek.peek-a-boo.app/app/early-childhood/classes)
Your actual rooms — name, capacity, age group, the teachers assigned to each, and what
appears on that room's daily report.
**Who normally uses it:** the principal or office administrator, at setup and when
staffing changes.

### [Curriculum](https://peek.peek-a-boo.app/app/early-childhood/curriculum)
The learning framework your nursery assesses against, and the top of the early-years
structure. Everything else in this group belongs to a curriculum.
**Who normally uses it:** the principal and the curriculum lead, at setup.

### [Age Groups](https://peek.peek-a-boo.app/app/early-childhood/age-groups)
The age bands your nursery uses, each defined by a range in months.
**Who normally uses it:** the principal and the curriculum lead, at setup.

### [Attainment](https://peek.peek-a-boo.app/app/early-childhood/attainment)
Your grading scale — the levels a teacher picks from when rating how securely a child has
grasped an objective.
**Who normally uses it:** the principal and the curriculum lead, at setup.

### [Learn Area](https://peek.peek-a-boo.app/app/early-childhood/learning-area)
The broad areas of learning within your curriculum, and the narrower **Aspects** inside
each one.
**Who normally uses it:** the principal and the curriculum lead, at setup.

### [Assessments](https://peek.peek-a-boo.app/app/early-childhood/assessment)
A small summary page with three counters and links through to the two pages where the
work actually happens.
**Who normally uses it:** principals and curriculum leads, for a quick overview.

### [Observations](https://peek.peek-a-boo.app/app/early-childhood/observation)
Where a teacher records what they saw a child do, and tags it against the objectives it
demonstrates.
**Who normally uses it:** teachers and room leads, through the week.

### [Assessment Report](https://peek.peek-a-boo.app/app/early-childhood/assessment-report)
The per-child, per-learning-area printable summary of progress that parents receive at
the end of a term.
**Who normally uses it:** teachers, room leads and principals, at the end of each term.

## Finance — money going out

### [Suppliers](https://peek.peek-a-boo.app/app/suppliers)
The address book of everyone you buy from or pay out to.
**Who normally uses it:** the accountant or office administrator.

### [Expenses](https://peek.peek-a-boo.app/app/expenses)
A cost the nursery has incurred, with the receipt or invoice scanned and attached.
**Who normally uses it:** the accountant.

### [Expense Types](https://peek.peek-a-boo.app/app/expense-types)
Your spending categories — Cleaning, Food, Stationery, Utilities, Maintenance.
**Who normally uses it:** the accountant or administrator, at setup.

### [Payment Voucher](https://peek.peek-a-boo.app/app/payment-voucher)
Money actually leaving the nursery to a supplier, split across one or more payment lines.
**Who normally uses it:** the accountant.

### [Purchases](https://peek.peek-a-boo.app/app/purchases)
What was ordered from a supplier, itemised line by line with quantities, fees and VAT.
**Who normally uses it:** the accountant or office administrator.

## Account — money coming in

### [Services](https://peek.peek-a-boo.app/app/services)
The catalogue of everything you charge for — tuition, transport, uniform — each with a
default fee and VAT treatment. Set this up before invoicing.
**Who normally uses it:** the accountant, principal or administrator, at setup.

### [Invoices](https://peek.peek-a-boo.app/app/invoices)
Billing a named child's family for one or more services.
**Who normally uses it:** the accountant, and sometimes the registrar or principal.

### [Receipts](https://peek.peek-a-boo.app/app/receipts)
Recording money received from a family.
**Who normally uses it:** the accountant, and reception when taking a payment.

### [Payment Modes](https://peek.peek-a-boo.app/app/payment-modes)
The ways money can move — cash, cheque, bank transfer, card — each mapped to an accounting
code. These must exist before a receipt or voucher can be saved.
**Who normally uses it:** the accountant or administrator, at setup.

### [Balance by Class](https://peek.peek-a-boo.app/app/balance-by-class)
The outstanding-fees view: everything **Billed** for a child, everything **Received** from
them, and the **Balance** between the two. This is the one page that answers "who owes us
money?".
**Who normally uses it:** the accountant and the principal.

## Reports & Admin

### [Attendance Reports](https://peek.peek-a-boo.app/app/attendance-reports)
Listed here as well, because it sits in this sidebar group. The read-only attendance
history for any past date range.
**Who normally uses it:** the principal, the office and guest accounts such as inspectors.

### [Roles & Access](https://peek.peek-a-boo.app/app/roles)
Where it is decided which parts of the portal each job role can open. Access is set per
role, never per person.
**Who normally uses it:** the system administrator alone.

## Admissions CRM — enquiries from new families

### [Leads](https://peek.peek-a-boo.app/app/crm/leads)
Every enquiry the nursery has received, from the first phone call through a tour to
enrolment, with its statuses, notes and follow-up tasks. Shown in the sidebar as
**CRM Leads**.
**Who normally uses it:** the registrar and admissions staff, with the principal looking
in.

### [Referrals & Sources](https://peek.peek-a-boo.app/app/crm/referrals-sources)
The two lookup lists recording where enquiries come from — how the family heard about you,
and which channel the enquiry arrived through. Both lists must have entries, because every
enquiry needs one of each.
**Who normally uses it:** the system administrator alone. Registrars ask the administrator
to add a new entry.
