# Training Tracks by Role

Peekaboo gives each member of staff a role, and your role decides which pages appear in your sidebar. This document gives each role its own training path: what you will use daily, what you will use now and then, what belongs to your colleagues, the order to learn things in, and how to tell when you are ready.

## How to use this document

Find your role, read that track from beginning to end, then work through its learning path in order. Each step points at a section of the how-to library, and the steps are sequenced so that each one builds on the last. Do not skip ahead — several tasks depend on set-up that an earlier step covers.

The portal itself is at **[https://peek.peek-a-boo.app](https://peek.peek-a-boo.app/login)**. Every page named in the tracks below has a direct link in *Every Page, One Click Away*, so you can open it while you read.

## Before you start — four things that apply to everyone

**1. Your sidebar is the honest answer.** The sidebar is the menu running down the side of the screen — the left-hand edge in English, the right-hand edge in Arabic. It lists exactly the pages you are allowed to open, and nothing else. Groups you have no access to disappear entirely, heading and all.

**2. These tracks describe the typical setup.** What each role can actually open is configured per nursery. Your administrator may have given your role more or less than the track below describes. If a page named in your track is not in your sidebar, it simply has not been enabled for your role here — ask your administrator, and treat your sidebar as the final word.

**3. The Principal sees everything.** The Principal role opens every page in the portal regardless of how anything else is configured.

**4. Two areas belong to specific roles and cannot be shared out.** Only the **Admin** can open **Roles & Access**, where what each role may see is decided, and only the Admin can open **Referrals & Sources**. Only the **Principal** can open the multi-centre **Centers** screen. Everyone else — including the Admin — manages their own nursery's details through **My Center**.

## A note on recording health, illness and injury

This applies to every role that touches a daily report, and it is the most important procedural point in this document.

The daily report form shows a **Health Status** selector and a **Temperature** box. **These two selections are not part of the saved report.** Whatever you pick there will not be in the record the parent reads or the record your colleagues see tomorrow.

So the correct procedure is:

1. **Write it in the Note to parents box on the daily report.** You will find this box in the **Teacher Note** section, towards the bottom of the report form. Anything about illness, injury, a temperature reading or a child being sent home goes in there, written out in words. That is the part that is saved and that the parent receives.
2. **Tell the room lead in person, the same day.** Do not rely on the portal to pass a health concern between staff.
3. **For anything ongoing** — an allergy, a medical condition, a special diet — record it on the child's own record. Open [Children](https://peek.peek-a-boo.app/app/children), open the child, then click the **Health** tab across the top of their record.
4. That is where long-term health information belongs and where it stays. It is also what raises the red allergy warning on the child's card.

Teach this as normal procedure from day one, to everyone who writes reports.

# Principal

## Who this is for

You run the nursery. You are accountable for whether it is full, whether it is safe, whether the books balance and whether families are happy. You spend your day moving between people, and you dip into the portal to check on things rather than to sit and process records. You also need to be able to open anything, anywhere, when somebody is away or something needs checking.

## What you will use every day

- **Live Dashboard** — your morning glance. Occupancy, attendance headline numbers, late arrivals and upcoming events on the **Main Dashboard** tab; billed against collected on the **Finance** tab; the admissions funnel on the **CRM** tab. It is the one page in the portal that refreshes its own figures while you watch.
- **Children Attendance** — who is actually on the premises right now.
- **Daily Reports** — spot-check that reports are being written and going out. This is quality control on the single thing parents judge you by.
- **CRM Leads** — the enquiries in flight and whether anybody is following them up.
- **Balance by Class** — who owes what.

## What you will use occasionally

- **Children** and **Staff** — the two rosters, when you need a record or a contact detail.
- **Messages** — nursery-wide announcements, closures, newsletters.
- **Calendar** — trips, parents' evenings, holidays.
- **Assessment Report** and **Assessments** — at the end of a term, and when a parent asks about progress.
- **Attendance Reports** — the history grid, for a past date or a pattern of absence.
- **Invoices**, **Receipts**, **Expenses** — oversight of the accountant's work.
- **My Center** — your nursery's own name, address, phone numbers, logo and session times.
- **Packages** — your nursery's own subscription to the Peekaboo software. This is not parent billing.
- **Centers** — the multi-centre screen, yours alone. This is where a new nursery on the platform is set up, together with the login for its administrator. Most principals open this rarely or never.

## What you probably will not see

Nothing — your role opens every page in the portal. That is a responsibility as much as a convenience. Because you can open the finance pages, the staff records and the admissions pipeline, be deliberate about which screens you leave open on a shared display, and be careful on pages like **Roles & Access** where a change affects everybody holding that role.

## Your learning path

1. **Get oriented.** Read *Welcome to Peekaboo* end to end, then sign in and complete *Your first 30 minutes*.
2. **Read the Live Dashboard** — see *How to read the Live Dashboard and switch its tabs*, then *How to change the dashboard's date range*.
3. **Understand the register** — see *How to mark a child present for today*, so you know what your team does each morning and what "today only" means.
4. **Look up a past date** — see *How to view (not edit) attendance for a past date* and *How to generate and export an attendance report*.
5. **Read a daily report** — see *How to review a daily report for a past date*, then read the health and temperature procedure at the top of this document.
6. **Watch the admissions pipeline** — see *How to change a lead's status* and *How to search, sort and filter the leads list*.
7. **Check the money** — see *How to view outstanding balances by class*, then *How to search and sort invoices or receipts*.
8. **Learn what a progress report contains** — see *How to generate an assessment report* and *How to export or print an assessment report*.
9. **Send a nursery-wide message** — see *How to message a whole class (or the whole nursery)*.
10. **Keep the centre record right** — see *How to update your centre's details* and *How to add a session (daily timing)*.
11. **Understand how access is decided** — see *How to change what a role can access*, so you can have an informed conversation with your administrator even though this is their day-to-day responsibility.
12. **Learn the multi-centre screen** — see *How to create a new centre*. Only you can open this, so nobody can cover it for you.

## You are ready when you can…

- Open the Live Dashboard and explain this morning's occupancy, late arrivals and outstanding balance to a colleague.
- Look up any child's attendance for a date last month.
- Open yesterday's daily report for any child and say whether it was published and emailed.
- State where a member of staff must record a temperature or an injury, and why the note to parents is the part that counts.
- Find every lead currently at the tour stage and say who is following each one up.
- Update your nursery's contact details and session times on **My Center** without help.

# Registrar

## Who this is for

You are the front desk and the face of admissions. You take the call when a parent rings about a place, you show families round, you chase the ones who went quiet, and when a family joins you are the one who turns them into a real child record with the right parent details, the right pickup people and the right class. Accuracy on your screens is what everything downstream depends on.

## What you will use every day

- **CRM Leads** — every enquiry, from the first phone call to the day the family enrols. Statuses, notes, follow-up tasks and the tour booking all live here.
- **Children** — the child roster, and behind it the largest form in the portal: the child's own details, parent and guardian details, health information, authorised pickup people, and the registration that puts the child in a class.
- **Children Attendance** — the front desk is often where arrivals and departures are actually recorded.
- **Live Dashboard** — the **CRM** tab for your funnel, the **Main** tab for today's numbers.

## What you will use occasionally

- **Classes** — to see which rooms exist, their age group and capacity, before registering a child into one.
- **Calendar** — booking open days, tours and events into the nursery calendar.
- **Messages** — sending information to a family or a class.
- **Attendance Reports** — when a parent asks about their child's attendance record.
- **Staff Attendance** — the same permission that opens the children's register also opens the staff clock-in sheet, so it may well be in your sidebar. Use it if front desk is where your team clocks in.
- **My Center** — if you are the person who keeps the nursery's contact details current.

## What you probably will not see

The **Finance** and **Account** groups — invoicing, receipts, suppliers and expenses belong to the accountant. The curriculum and assessment pages beyond Classes belong to teachers and whoever leads on curriculum. **Roles & Access** and **Referrals & Sources** belong to the administrator — so if a new "how did you hear about us?" option is needed, that is a request you make to them rather than something you add yourself. **Centers** belongs to the principal; your nursery's own details are on **My Center**.

## Your learning path

1. **Get oriented.** Read *Welcome to Peekaboo*, sign in, and complete *Your first 30 minutes*.
2. **Learn the table controls once** — see *How to use the standard table controls (search, sort, page size, paging)*. Every list you touch works this way.
3. **Take an enquiry** — see *How to add a new lead (enquiry)*, then *How to edit a lead*.
4. **Work the list** — see *How to search, sort and filter the leads list*.
5. **Book a visit** — see *How to book and record a nursery tour*.
6. **Keep the history** — see *How to add a note to a lead*, then *How to create a task (follow-up) on a lead*. Nothing in the portal chases a family for you; the task is your reminder and it is only as good as your habit of setting one.
7. **Move the lead along** — see *How to change a lead's status*, then *How to record that a lead has been accepted* and *How to mark a lead as lost or rejected*.
8. **Attribute the enquiry** — see *How to attach a referral or source to a lead*.
9. **Create the child** — see *How to move an accepted family into enrolment*, then *How to add a new child*. Note that an accepted lead does not become a child record on its own — creating the child is your step, and it is the one most often forgotten.
10. **Complete the family record** — see *How to add a parent / guardian*, then *How to add an authorised pickup person*, then *How to record an allergy, medical condition or special diet*.
11. **Put the child in a room** — see *How to register a child into a class*.
12. **Keep the roster clean** — see *How to search and filter the children list*, then *How to edit a child's details* and *How to deactivate or delete a child*.

## You are ready when you can…

- Take a phone enquiry and have it recorded as a lead, with a source and a follow-up task, before the call ends.
- Book a tour for a family and find it again a week later.
- Turn an accepted family into a complete child record: child, parent, pickup people, health information and a class registration.
- List every lead that has gone quiet for more than two weeks.
- Find any child on the roster in a few seconds and confirm who is authorised to collect them.
- Explain to a parent how their child's daily report reaches them.

# Teacher

## Who this is for

You run a room. Your day is the children in front of you, and the portal exists to take three jobs off your shoulders: the register, what you tell parents, and the evidence of each child's learning. Everything in your track is something you do while the room is running, so speed and habit matter more than depth.

## What you will use every day

- **Children Attendance** — marking your class present, absent, late or excused, and checking children out at the end of the day. This page works on **today only**, so mark it as the morning happens.
- **Daily Reports** — the care record that goes home: mood, meals and drinks, nap times, milk, nappies, items needed, and your note to parents.
- **Observations** — what you saw a child do, tagged against the objectives it demonstrates.
- **Children** — looking up a child's details, allergies or who is authorised to collect them.

## What you will use occasionally

- **Assessments** — the overview of how your class is progressing.
- **Assessment Report** — the per-child, per-learning-area printable summary at the end of a term.
- **Messages** — sending a note, photo or video to your class's parents.
- **Calendar** — trips and events for your room.
- **Classes** — seeing your room's setup and which rows appear on its daily report.
- **Attendance Reports** — when you need a past date rather than today.
- **Staff Attendance** — the same permission that opens the children's register also opens the staff clock-in sheet, so it may appear in your sidebar.

## What you probably will not see

The **Finance** and **Account** groups belong to the accountant. **CRM Leads** belongs to the registrar. **Staff** records, **Roles & Access** and **Referrals & Sources** belong to administration. The curriculum framework pages — **Curriculum**, **Learn Area**, **Age Groups**, **Attainment** — are usually set up once by whoever leads on curriculum, and you work inside the framework they built rather than changing it.

## Your learning path

1. **Get oriented.** Read *Welcome to Peekaboo*, sign in, and complete *Your first 30 minutes*.
2. **Mark your class present** — see *How to mark a child present for today*.
3. **Handle the exceptions** — see *How to mark a child absent or excused*, then *How to correct a child's attendance times or notes (today)*.
4. **See only your room** — see *How to filter the attendance board by classroom*.
5. **Close the day** — see *How to check a child out at the end of the day*.
6. **Write a report** — see *How to create and save a daily report as a draft*. Before you do, read the health and temperature procedure at the top of this document, and get into the habit of writing the **Note to parents** box first.
7. **Send it home** — see *How to publish a daily report (without emailing it)*, then *How to publish a daily report and email it to the parent in one step*. Learn the difference: publishing marks it final, emailing is what reaches the parent.
8. **Fix and re-send** — see *How to edit the teacher note on a report already sent*, then *How to email an already-saved daily report*.
9. **Look back** — see *How to review a daily report for a past date*.
10. **Record what you saw** — see *How to record an observation*, then *How to link objectives to an observation*.
11. **Maintain your evidence** — see *How to view, edit or delete a saved observation*.
12. **Report on progress** — see *How to read the Assessments summary page*, then *How to generate an assessment report*, *How to edit comments on a saved assessment report* and *How to export or print an assessment report*.

## You are ready when you can…

- Mark your whole class present, absent or late within a few minutes of the morning settling.
- Write, publish and email a complete daily report for one child without checking the steps.
- State exactly where a temperature, an illness or a bump goes — in the note to parents, and to the room lead in person.
- Record an observation and tag it against at least one objective at the right attainment level.
- Produce and print an end-of-term assessment report for a child in your room.
- Look up any child's allergies and authorised pickup people while standing at the door.

# Assistant

## Who this is for

You support a room alongside the teacher. In the portal your day-to-day work looks much like theirs — the register and the daily reports — but you will usually be given a narrower set of pages, focused on recording what happens in the room rather than on assessment and reporting. You are very often the person who actually enters the day's detail, so the quality of what parents read depends on you.

## What you will use every day

- **Children Attendance** — marking the room present, absent, late or excused, and checking children out. **Today only**; mark it as it happens.
- **Daily Reports** — the care record: mood, meals and drinks, nap times, milk, nappies, items needed, and the note to parents.
- **Children** — looking up allergies, dietary needs and who may collect a child.

## What you will use occasionally

- **Calendar** — what is happening in the room this week.
- **Messages** — where your nursery has you sending photos or notes to parents.
- **Observations** — where your nursery includes assistants in recording observations.
- **Attendance Reports** — looking up a past date.
- **Live Dashboard** — the day's headline numbers.

## What you probably will not see

The **Finance** and **Account** groups, **CRM Leads**, **Staff** records and the administration pages all belong to other roles. The curriculum framework and assessment reporting pages usually sit with the teacher and the curriculum lead. Your track is deliberately narrow — it is the part of the portal that runs the room.

## Your learning path

1. **Get oriented.** Read *Welcome to Peekaboo*, sign in, and complete *Your first 30 minutes*.
2. **Mark the room present** — see *How to mark a child present for today*.
3. **Handle the exceptions** — see *How to mark a child absent or excused*.
4. **Correct a mistake** — see *How to correct a child's attendance times or notes (today)*.
5. **See only your room** — see *How to filter the attendance board by classroom*.
6. **Close the day** — see *How to check a child out at the end of the day*.
7. **Learn the health procedure** — read the health and temperature note at the top of this document before you write a single report.
8. **Write a report** — see *How to create and save a daily report as a draft*. Fill the **Note to parents** box first, every time.
9. **Send it home** — see *How to publish a daily report (without emailing it)*, then *How to publish a daily report and email it to the parent in one step*.
10. **Look back** — see *How to review a daily report for a past date*.
11. **Know the children** — see *How to search and filter the children list*, then *How to record an allergy, medical condition or special diet* so you can read a child's health record confidently.

## You are ready when you can…

- Mark the room present and handle absences without asking for help.
- Complete a full daily report for a child, including a clear note to parents.
- Say out loud where a temperature or an injury is recorded, and who you tell in person.
- Check a child out correctly at home time.
- Find a child's allergies and authorised pickup people in seconds.

# Accountant

## Who this is for

You keep the books. Fees going out to families, money coming back in, suppliers being paid, costs being recorded and categorised, and a clear view of who is in arrears. Most of your work is in two sidebar groups — **Account** for money in, **Finance** for money out — and a few catalogue pages that need setting up once and maintaining thereafter.

## What you will use every day

- **Invoices** — billing a named child for one or more services.
- **Receipts** — recording money received from a family.
- **Balance by Class** — what each child still owes, worked out as billed minus received. This is your arrears list.
- **Expenses** — costs incurred, with the receipt scan attached and a category against each.
- **Live Dashboard** — the **Finance** tab, for billed against collected and outstanding balances.

## What you will use occasionally

- **Services** — the catalogue of billable things: tuition, transport, uniform, each with a default fee and VAT treatment.
- **Payment Modes** — the tender types you accept, such as cash, cheque and transfer.
- **Expense Types** — the categories your costs are filed under.
- **Suppliers** — the vendor list.
- **Purchases** — what was ordered, line by line.
- **Payment Voucher** — money actually leaving the bank, split across payment lines.
- **Children** — to confirm a child's details before invoicing.
- **My Center** — the VAT number and the bank or payment details parents see.
- **Packages** — the nursery's own software subscription, if that is yours to manage.

Two points worth knowing before you start. **Suppliers, Purchases, Expenses and Payment Voucher are four independent records that happen to share a supplier** — a purchase does not flow into an expense, and an expense does not flow into a voucher. Record each one where it belongs. And payment is reconciled at the **child** level, in **Balance by Class**, rather than invoice by invoice — so a family's position is the total billed minus the total received, and that is the figure to quote when a parent asks what they owe.

## What you probably will not see

**CRM Leads** belongs to the registrar. The daily reports, observations, assessments and curriculum pages belong to the teaching team. **Staff** records, **Roles & Access** and **Referrals & Sources** belong to administration. **Centers** belongs to the principal.

Note one wide door in the other direction: because the arrears view sits alongside invoices and receipts, several colleagues may be able to see every child's balance. Treat that screen as sensitive in a shared office.

## Your learning path

1. **Get oriented.** Read *Welcome to Peekaboo*, sign in, and complete *Your first 30 minutes*.
2. **Learn the table controls once** — see *How to use the standard table controls (search, sort, page size, paging)*, then *How to export a finance table (Copy / PDF / Print / CSV)*. You will use both constantly.
3. **Build the fee catalogue** — see *How to add a service*. Do this before any invoicing; an invoice bills services, so the catalogue has to exist first.
4. **Set up how you take money** — see *How to add a payment mode*.
5. **Raise a bill** — see *How to create an invoice*.
6. **Send it** — see *How to print or email an invoice*.
7. **Take the money** — see *How to record a payment (create a receipt)*, then *How to print or email a receipt*.
8. **Reconcile** — see *How to view outstanding balances by class*, and read *How to mark an invoice as paid* to understand why a family's position is read at the child level rather than per invoice.
9. **Find anything again** — see *How to search and sort invoices or receipts*.
10. **Set up the cost side** — see *How to add a supplier*, then *How to create an expense type*.
11. **Record what you spend** — see *How to record an expense*, attaching the receipt scan.
12. **Order and pay** — see *How to raise a purchase*, then *How to create a payment voucher*.

## You are ready when you can…

- Add a new service with the correct fee and VAT, and use it on an invoice the same day.
- Invoice a child, email the invoice, record the receipt when the family pays, and confirm the balance has moved.
- Produce this month's arrears list from **Balance by Class** and export it.
- Record an expense with its receipt attached and the right expense type.
- Raise a purchase against a supplier and record the payment voucher when the money leaves.
- Explain the difference between the nursery's own software subscription under **Packages** and the fees you bill to parents.

# Admin

## Who this is for

You are the system administrator for the nursery. You are the person colleagues come to when they cannot see a page they need, when a new member of staff needs an account, or when the enquiry source list needs a new entry. Your job is less about daily records and more about making sure everybody else's portal is shaped correctly.

Two areas belong to you alone: **Roles & Access**, where what each role can open is decided, and **Referrals & Sources**, the "how did you hear about us?" lists. No amount of configuration can give those to another role, so if you are away, nobody can cover them.

## What you will use every day

- **Roles & Access** — the roles in your nursery and the modules each one may open.
- **Staff** — the employee roster, where accounts are created, roles are assigned, and passwords are set.
- **Live Dashboard** — the general picture.

## What you will use occasionally

- **Referrals & Sources** — maintaining the enquiry source lists that the registrar attaches to leads.
- **My Center** — your nursery's name, address, contact details, logo, VAT details and session times.
- **Packages** — the nursery's own subscription to the Peekaboo software.
- **Children** — when you need a record for support purposes.
- **Attendance Reports** — for a compliance or inspection request.
- **Calendar** and **Messages** — where you handle nursery-wide communication.

## What you probably will not see

The daily teaching pages — daily reports, observations and assessments — belong to the teaching team, and the invoicing and expense pages belong to the accountant, unless your nursery has specifically given you those too. **Centers**, the multi-centre screen, belongs to the principal alone; your own nursery's settings are on **My Center**.

One important limit to know about your own role: being the Admin does **not** by itself give you every other page. Beyond your two exclusive screens, you see what has been enabled for your role like anybody else.

## Four things to internalise before you change anything

1. **Permissions are per role, never per person.** Changing a role changes what every single person holding that role can see. If only one person should get something, create a role for that purpose and move them to it.
2. **Other people do not pick up your change until they sign out and sign back in.** Your own changes apply to you immediately, which is why a change can look like it worked for you and not for them. Always tell the person to sign out and back in before concluding anything.
3. **A brand-new role starts with nothing selected.** Anyone assigned to it will be able to open almost nothing until you grant its modules. Create the role and grant its access in one sitting.
4. **Some access is decided by which role a person holds, not by ticking modules.** **Roles & Access** and **Referrals & Sources** require the Admin role. **Centers** requires the Principal role. Ticking boxes will never grant those three — the person needs the role itself, which is set on their **Staff** record.

## Your learning path

1. **Get oriented.** Read *Welcome to Peekaboo*, sign in, and complete *Your first 30 minutes*.
2. **Understand how access works from the user's side** — see *How to find out what your role is and what you can access*. Every support question you get starts here.
3. **Create a staff account** — see *How to add a staff member (and create their portal login)*. This is where a person's role is set.
4. **Maintain the roster** — see *How to edit a staff member*, then *How to search and filter the staff list, and switch card/table view*.
5. **Reset a password** — see *How to change a staff member's password*. Note that staff cannot change their own password inside the portal; their only self-service route is the **Forgot?** link on the login screen. Treat the Staff page as sensitive, because passwords are visible there.
6. **Handle the "no role" case** — see *What to do if you see "Your role could not be determined"*. This is the single most common new-starter problem, and the fix is on the Staff record.
7. **Create a role** — see *How to create a role*.
8. **Grant its access** — see *How to change what a role can access*. Do this immediately after step 7.
9. **Answer "I can't see X"** — see *How to give a user access to a module*. Work out the module, find the person's role, grant it, and have them sign out and back in.
10. **Retire a role safely** — see *How to deactivate or delete a role*. Check the **Staff** page first to see who holds it; the portal will not warn you about the people affected, and the status toggle acts on a single click with no confirmation.
11. **Maintain the CRM lists** — see *How to add a referral or a communication source*. Only you can do this, so agree a turnaround with your registrar.
12. **Keep the centre record right** — see *How to update your centre's details*, then *How to add a session (daily timing)*. Sessions matter beyond settings: they are the timing options the registrar picks from when registering a child into a class, so if that list is empty, this is the fix.
13. **Manage the subscription** — see *How to buy or renew a subscription package*, if that is yours.
14. **Deactivate a leaver** — see *How to deactivate or delete a staff member*.

## You are ready when you can…

- Create a new member of staff with a working login and the correct role, and talk them through their first sign-in.
- Create a role and grant it exactly the pages that job needs.
- Take "I can't see the Invoices page" from report to resolution, including telling the person to sign out and back in.
- Explain why some access cannot be granted by ticking a module, and what has to change instead.
- Add a new referral source and confirm the registrar can attach it to a lead.
- Add an active session and confirm it appears as a timing option when a child is registered into a class.
- Say, without looking it up, that permissions are per role and never per person.

# Guest

## Who this is for

You have been given a deliberately limited account — perhaps you are a visiting inspector, an auditor, a trustee, or someone joining for a short project. You have been granted a small, specific set of pages for a specific purpose, and you are expected to look rather than to change.

Of all the roles, this is the one where local variation matters most. There is no standard guest setup; your nursery decides exactly what your account can open, and it may be a single page.

## What you will use every day

Whatever has been enabled for your visit. In practice a guest account is most often given some combination of:

- **Live Dashboard** — headline occupancy, attendance and finance figures.
- **Attendance Reports** — the read-only attendance history across a date range, for children or staff.
- One or two further pages relevant to why you are here.

## What you will use occasionally

Very little. If your work needs a page you do not have, ask your host rather than trying to reach it another way — it will not open.

## What you probably will not see

Most of the portal. The children's and staff records, the daily reports, the curriculum and assessment pages, the invoicing and expense pages, the admissions pipeline and all of the administration screens belong to the nursery's own staff. Your narrow menu is the account working exactly as intended.

## Your learning path

1. **Get oriented.** Read the *What Peekaboo is* and *What a normal day looks like* sections of *Welcome to Peekaboo*, so you understand the system you are looking at.
2. **Sign in** — see *How to sign in to the portal*.
3. **Find your role and your scope** — see *How to find out what your role is and what you can access*. Read your sidebar carefully; it is the complete list of what your visit covers.

# If your account has no role yet

Occasionally a brand-new account exists before a role has been set on it. If that is you, you will sign in successfully but see a message telling you that your role could not be determined, with a short grey line of detail beneath it.

This simply means the setup of your account is not finished. Nothing is wrong with your password and nothing is lost.

**What to do:**

1. Note the small grey line of detail shown beneath the message.
2. Send it to your administrator and ask them to set your role.
3. Once they confirm it is done, **sign out and sign back in**.

Signing out and back in on its own will not fix it — the role genuinely has to be set first. Once it is, you will land on the Live Dashboard normally, and you can start on the track above that matches your job.
